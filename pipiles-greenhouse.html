Why buy from a grower, not a garden department?

When plants are grown a few miles from where they'll live, they're already used to our Niagara-shoreline weather — no shock from a cross-country truck, no picked-over end-of-week racks. And the person ringing you up actually grew the thing and can tell you how to keep it alive.

  • Grown on the premises — not shipped in and marked up
  • Hardened to local weather — ready for your garden, not a warehouse
  • Real growing advice — ask the family, they raised it
  • Fresh & unpicked-over — new plants coming on all season
  • A local fixture — the greenhouse Newfane drives to every spring
