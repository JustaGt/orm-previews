Three families have kept this counter since 1860. Your boots are behind it.

Carhartt jackets and overalls, Muck and Carolina boots, sturdy clothing for work and every day — sized, stocked, and sold in person at 147 Main Street, the way it's been done in Moravia for 165 years.

The store ledger165 years, one storefront, three families

1860

Jennings opens as Moravia's dry-goods and clothing store, in the decade before the transcontinental railroad.

1870s

The store moves into its present building at 147 Main Street — and never leaves.

Today

Mark and Lynette Wood, the third family to keep the store, stock it for the people who actually shop here: farmers, tradespeople, and families from every corner of southern Cayuga County.

Brands that earn their shelf space

A store doesn't last 165 years selling things that fall apart. The racks carry working brands, in working sizes.

On the racksWhat you'll find at 147 Main

Workwear

Carhartt duck jackets, bib overalls, and thermal layers for barn, site, and woodlot — the department the county knows us for.

Footwear

Muck, Carolina, and Timberland boots, plus casual shoes and sandals. Bring your worn pair; we'll match the fit.

Everyday clothing

Practical men's, women's, and children's clothing at fair prices — no trip to Auburn required.

"One of the last of a dying breed."— The Citizen (Auburn), on Jennings' place among New York's surviving small-town department stores

Recognized by the New York State Senate in 2010 for a century and a half of service to Moravia — and listed by travelers as a destination worth the drive off the Cayuga Lake byway.
