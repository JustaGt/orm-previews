Three generations rooted in Aquebogue

The Stakey family has farmed Aquebogue since the early 1900s — Jim's grandfather and father grew potatoes, cauliflower, strawberries and lima beans on this ground. In 1976 Jim opened one of Long Island's first pick-your-own pumpkin farms, and in 1978 he started growing flowers for the wholesale trade.

In 1998 the operation expanded with a modern Venlo glass greenhouse — the controlled environment that lets us hold quality from cutting to finished basket.

Buyers who've worked with us know the difference a real grower makes: plants that were fed and spaced and hardened correctly, so they don't stall the week after they hit your shelf.

That's the whole pitch. We grow it here, under glass, and we grow it to sell.
