What comes across the counter

Most jobs are done the same day. Pull the old hose off the machine, bring it in, and we match the length, ends and pressure rating from stock.

Hoses made on the spot

Blown line off a loader, skidder, dump trailer or planter? We build the replacement to your old one — crimped, tagged and pressure-matched while you wait.

Fittings & adapters

One of the deepest walls of fittings in the region — metric and British, JIC, ORB, pipe and flare — so the odd end on an old European or farm machine isn't a special order.

Forklift repair

Lift cylinders, mast hoses, hydraulics and general service on warehouse and yard forklifts, kept running for the shops and distributors around Plattsburgh.

Machine shop work

Turning, cylinder rebuilds, pins, bushings and small fabrication — the kind of one-off metal work the dealership won't touch on a 30-year-old machine.

Who pulls in the lot
