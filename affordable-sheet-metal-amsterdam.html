What we fabricate

Cut, formed and welded to your dimensions

A lot of duct and metal work is close-enough off a supply-house shelf — until it isn't. When the fitting has to match a real opening, a tight chase or an oddball angle, that's the work we do. Bring the measurements, we'll build the part.

01

HVAC duct & plenums

Custom rectangular and round duct, plenums, transitions, offsets and takeoffs sized to the job — not forced to fit a stock piece.

02

Fittings & transitions

Elbows, boots, reducers and the odd-angle transition that connects two things that were never meant to line up.

03

Flashing & trim metal

Custom flashing, drip edge, counter-flashing and bent trim for roofers, builders and remodelers in the gauges you need.

04

Guards, brackets & enclosures

Short-run fabricated brackets, machine guards, covers and enclosures — welded and finished, ready to bolt up.

05

Welding & repair

Sheet and light-plate welding for new pieces or repairs — steel, stainless and aluminum, in the shop or brought together for assembly.

06

One-off & custom orders

The piece a homeowner or shop can't buy anywhere: a replacement panel, a custom cover, a one-of part built from a sketch.

In-house powder coating

Fabricated and finished under one roof

Most shops send parts out to be coated — which means a second vendor, a second lead time and a second markup. We powder coat in-house, so a bracket or guard gets built and finished in one place. Parts up to about 7 feet, in the color and finish the job calls for.
