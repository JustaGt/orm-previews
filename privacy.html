The fine print, in plain words

Privacy and text terms

Last updated October 2026

By Yurii’s Pizza is a food truck at 901 N Washington St, Junction City, KS 66441. This page explains what we collect when you use our website, order online, book the truck or sign up for texts, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, mobile number, email if you give it, what you ordered, your pickup time and any notes.
  • When you book the truck: your name, phone, email, event date, location, guest count and anything you tell us about the event.
  • Miles for a slice map: if you choose to add a pin, your first name or initials and the town you came from.
  • Text list: your mobile number and the fact that you agreed to get texts.
  • If you pay online: payment is handled by Stripe on their secure page. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To make your order, have it ready on time and reach you if something comes up.
  • To plan and confirm your event and the deposit.
  • To send texts only if you signed up for them.
  • To show your first name and town on our map, only after you add a pin and we approve it.

We don’t sell your information. We don’t share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Text messages

If you check the box or sign up on our site, By Yurii’s Pizza will text you about specials, new menu items and where the truck is parked. Message frequency varies. Msg and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP any time to stop getting texts. Reply HELP for help, or call us at 785.556.8303. Agreeing to texts is never required to order.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Order and booking confirmations may also come by text or email. Those are about the order you placed, not marketing.

Services that help us run the site

These companies process information for us only to do their job for our business:

  • GoHighLevel (LeadConnector): receives orders, booking requests and text list sign ups, and sends our emails and texts.
  • Stripe: handles online payments and event deposits.
  • Google: holds our truck schedule and map pins, and shows the map on our site.
  • Cloudflare: runs the small server that checks prices on online orders.
  • OpenStreetMap: map tiles for the Miles for a slice map.

If you order through DoorDash or Uber Eats, their own privacy policies apply to those orders.

On your phone

Our website remembers what’s in your cart and your name and number for checkout on your own device, so you don’t lose your order if you refresh. You can clear it any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Kids

Our website isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

Want us to delete your information, take down your map pin or take you off our text list? Call or text 785.556.8303 or email byyuriiskitchen@gmail.com and we’ll take care of it.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
