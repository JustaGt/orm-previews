Never bought a quarter of beef? Here's how it works

Buying beef by the share is cheaper per pound and fills a freezer — but it's confusing the first time. It's really just four steps.

1

Pick your share

Quarter, half or whole. A quarter suits a small family; a half or whole is better value per pound for bigger freezers.

2

Reserve with a deposit

We put your name on an animal. The balance is figured on hanging weight when it's processed. [Deposit & pricing to be confirmed.]

3

Choose your cuts

You fill out a cut sheet — steak thickness, roast sizes, how much ground, bacon options. We'll walk you through it.

4

Pick up & freeze

Your beef comes back cut, wrapped and frozen, ready to load into the freezer.
