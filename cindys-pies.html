Since 1978

Forty-seven years of flour on the same floorboards.

Cindy's opened its Dutch door in 1978, when grape pie was still a hometown secret traded at church suppers. Naples grew into the grape pie capital of the world, and the little bakery on Academy Street kept doing the only thing it ever did: real fruit, scratch pastry, weekend batches.

No franchise, no food-service filling, no shortcuts — just the pie that made this valley's name, from people who've been baking it longer than most of the wineries on the trail have been pouring.
