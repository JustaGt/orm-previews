While you're here

A quiet base for the whole Genesee valley

Angelica is one of the prettiest villages in the Southern Tier — a National-Register park circle, antique shops, and easy roads out to bigger adventures.

Historic Angelica

Stroll the Park Circle historic district, the shops, and the county fairgrounds just minutes from your site.

Letchworth & the falls

The "Grand Canyon of the East" and the Genesee River gorge are an easy day trip north.

Allegany County backroads

Fishing, hiking, fall color and the Allegany Artisan trail across the surrounding hills.
