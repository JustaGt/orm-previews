Family Farm Market & Greenhouse · Shortsville, NY

Plants in spring, sweet corn in summer, your deer in November — same family, same stand on Route 96.

Serendipity Acres is a working farm market run by a local family. Year-round beef, pork, chicken and eggs; a greenhouse full of plants come spring; firewood and bulk topsoil, stone and gravel when you need a load — and deer & wild-game processing every fall.

What you'll find when you pull in

Most farm stands do one thing. This one grew into five. Here's the whole place at a glance — so you know before you drive out whether we've got what you came for.

The Market

Local meat & pantry

All-natural beef, pork and chicken, farm eggs, plus honey, maple syrup, cheese, baked goods and homemade soaps from around the Finger Lakes. Stocked year round.

The Greenhouse

Spring plants & starts

Vegetable starts, annuals, hanging baskets and plants for the garden, home and landscaping — grown and cared for on-site from early spring into early fall.

Fall Service

Deer & wild-game processing

Seasonal deer processing and custom wild-game butchering, including custom sausage. Bring it in cut-and-wrapped the way you want it.

The Woodpile

Firewood

Firewood for the stove and the fire pit. Ask about seasoned availability and delivery to your place.

Bulk Yard

Topsoil, stone & gravel

Bulk materials sold and delivered — topsoil, stone, gravel and more, with excavating and trucking when the job calls for it.

Seasonal

Produce in season

Homegrown and locally sourced produce through the summer and fall — sweet corn, tomatoes and whatever's coming ripe that week.

A year at Serendipity Acres

There's a reason to stop by in every season

The stand changes with the calendar. Here's roughly what's going on when — so you can plan the trip.

Spring

Greenhouse opens

Vegetable starts, annuals and baskets for the garden and the porch. Come early for the best selection.

Summer

Produce & the market

Sweet corn, tomatoes and the day's picking, alongside the year-round meat and pantry case.

Fall

Deer processing

Deer season means the processing side gets busy — reserve ahead so you're not waiting in the rush.

Winter

Firewood & meat

Firewood for the cold months and a full meat case for the holidays. Bulk materials by appointment.

Deer & wild-game processing

Bring us your deer — cut and wrapped your way

Every fall we take in deer and other wild game for custom processing, including our own sausage. Because it gets busy fast once the season opens, the smartest move is to call ahead and get your name in before the November rush. Tell us how you want it cut, and it'll be ready to pick up clean, labeled and ready for the freezer.

Get on the deer list →

Drop-off hours, pricing and cut options are set each season — call to confirm this year's details.

A newer chapter, same country stand

Serendipity Acres is family-owned and run by Nathan Boor and Conor Trippe, who took the place over and put their name on it. The idea is simple: keep it local, keep it honest, and be the one stop on Route 96 that can hand you a plant flat, a pound of ground beef and a load of topsoil in the same visit.
