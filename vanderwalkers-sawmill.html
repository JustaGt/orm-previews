What we cut and sell

A working bandmill sells specifics — so here's what actually comes off the carriage. Species and sizes on hand change through the year; send what you're after and we'll tell you what's ready.

🪵

Live-edge slabs

Rough-sawn slabs for tables, benches, mantels and bar tops. Tell us the length and thickness you want and we'll cut to it or pull what's already drying on the rack.

📏

Dimensional lumber

Rough-sawn boards and timbers cut to your dimensions — the sizes a big-box aisle doesn't carry, for barns, decks, fences and framing.

❄️

Stakes

Snowplow markers and landscape stakes by the bundle — a staple for plow crews and landscapers heading into the season.

🏠

Log siding, flooring & trim

Milled and planed log siding, flooring and trim to finish a build or a cabin — cut from real boards, not ordered off a truck.
