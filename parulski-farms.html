Why buy it here

"You can come and see exactly where your food is sourced. That's the whole point of the farm."

The beef is finished on forage we grew a field over. The turkeys and chickens are out on pasture where you can watch them. Nothing about how the meat is raised is a mystery — you're welcome to walk the ground it came from.

Our standing rule

No hormones. No antibiotics. On every animal we raise.

Healthy food the community can count on — that's the number-one goal of the farm, and it hasn't changed.
