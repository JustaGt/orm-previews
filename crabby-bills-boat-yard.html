why Silver Lake

The quiet lake next to the big canyon

Silver Lake is the calm, family-sized lake in Wyoming County — small enough to feel like your own, close enough to make a real day of it.

You're ten minutes from Letchworth State Park, the "Grand Canyon of the East," so it's easy to spend the morning on the water and the afternoon at the falls.

We've been the screen-door, life-jacket-and-bait kind of boat yard on this shore for years — formerly Mack's, now Crabby Bill's.
