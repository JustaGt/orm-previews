Why this store exists

The block burned. The village didn't.

In January 2025, a fire took out an entire block of Ovid's Main Street — including the Big M, the only full grocery for miles. For over a year, "running to the store" meant driving twenty miles. Hearth & Harvest opened in spring 2026 to put fresh food back within walking distance, stocked from the farms and kitchens that were here all along.

"This has helped a lot — the fresh meats, and some produce in there as well." — Ovid Local Development Corporation board member, on the store's opening (WHAM-TV, May 2026)

Between the lakes

On the wine trail? Ovid is your halfway stop.

Route 96 runs straight through the village, midway between Seneca and Cayuga lakes. Stock the cottage kitchen, grab lunch for the tasting-room loop, or just get a real cup of coffee before the next stop.
