What you'll find on the hill

More than a tree — a morning out here.

Cut-Your-Own Fields

Fraser fir, balsam fir and blue spruce planted in walkable rows across the ridge. Handsaws at the barn — take your time and pick the shape that fits your ceiling.

Fresh-Cut & Pre-Cut

Don't want to hike in the cold? We keep a stand of fresh-cut trees by the barn, cut here on the farm — not shipped in on a truck.

Shake, Bale & Tie

Every tree gets mechanically shaken clean of loose needles, baled tight in netting, and tied onto your vehicle before you leave.

Wreaths & Roping

Hand-made balsam wreaths and garland roping for the door, the mantel and the porch rail — made from our own boughs.

Wholesale Cuts

Selling trees at your own lot, church or fundraiser? We cut wholesale quantities to order — call early in the fall to reserve.

Bring the Kids (& the Dog)

Open fields, farm dogs to greet you, and the corgis the farm's known for. It's the kind of trip that becomes the family's yearly tradition.

How cut-your-own works

Four steps, start to strapped-on-the-roof.

Grab a sawStop at the barn, say hi, and pick up a handsaw and a tree cart.
Walk the rowsHead into the fields and find the fir or spruce that's the right height and fullness.
Cut it lowSaw it close to the ground — we'll show first-timers exactly how.
We finish itBack at the barn we shake, bale and tie it on. You pay and you're home for cocoa.

Why folks drive out here

Fifty years on the same hill.

Plenty of places will sell you a tree. Dog Hollow grows them — from seedling to the one you cut — on 300 rolling acres the family has worked for half a century. The trees are fresher because they were standing this morning, the trip is the memory kids ask for every December, and yes, the corgis will supervise. Come out from Cortland, Homer, Truxton or DeRuyter and make an afternoon of it.
