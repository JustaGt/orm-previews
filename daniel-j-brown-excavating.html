What we dig

Site & earthwork for rural Otsego County

From a new house pad on a wooded lot to a driveway that finally drains right — the dirt work that has to be done before anything else can.

Site Prep & House Pads

Clearing, stripping, cut-and-fill and pad work to get a building lot ready before the foundation crew shows up.

Driveways & Roads

New driveways, camp roads and re-grades — proper base, crown and ditching so they shed water instead of holding it.

Foundations & Footings

Digging and backfill for foundations, footings, additions and outbuildings, cut to grade and ready for the pour.

Drainage & Wet Lots

Curtain drains, culverts, swales and regrading to move water off a wet yard, basement or driveway for good.

Septic Site Work

Excavation and site work for new and replacement septic systems — tank, field and the grading around them.

Clearing & Grading

Land clearing, stump and brush removal, pond digging and finish grading to shape a lot the way you want it.
