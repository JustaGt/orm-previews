A working waterfront name

When the town is named Oyster Bay, the oysters had better be real.

Frank M. Flower & Sons has dredged the leased grounds of Oyster Bay and Mill Neck Creek since 1887 — through booms, busts, storms and seasons that closed every other outfit around them.

Today they are the last commercial oyster- and clam-dredging company left on Long Island. The dredge boats still go out. The family still runs it.

That's not a tagline. It's a hundred and thirty-odd years of the same family pulling shellfish from the same bay.
