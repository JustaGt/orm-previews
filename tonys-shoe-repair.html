A Westfield specialty

The glove doctor of Portage Street

Every spring the gloves come in — laces snapped, pockets gone soft, palms dried stiff in a garage over winter. Tony relaces and restores them by hand, the same way he's done for decades. Little-league seasons have been saved on this bench.

Heading to a tournament? Drop the glove early in the week — restored gloves are typically ready before Saturday's first pitch. Call ahead to be sure.
Since your grandfather's boots

Three generations at the bench

The Franchina family has repaired Westfield's footwear across three generations — through grape harvests, factory shifts, and every winter Route 20 could throw at a pair of boots. Forty-five years of hands-on repair experience means Tony has almost certainly fixed one just like yours.

Around the village they don't say "find a cobbler." They say take it to Tony.
