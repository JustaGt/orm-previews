Talk

The shop

A small bench with three decades of habits

Barensfeld Cabinets has worked out of Chatham since 1994 — a small shop by choice, a member of the Berkshire Woodworkers Guild, building for homes across Columbia County and the Berkshires.

Small means the person who quotes your job is the person who builds it. It also means we take on the work we can do properly, and tell you when something isn't worth the bench time.
