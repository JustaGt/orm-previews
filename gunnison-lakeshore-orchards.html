Six generations, one orchard

Gunnison Lakeshore Orchards has grown apples on this stretch of the Lake Champlain shore since 1826. Six generations of the same family have worked these 165 acres — pruning in the cold months, picking in the fall, and baking what doesn't go home in a bag.

It's a working farm and a fall tradition for families across the Champlain Valley and the Adirondacks.
