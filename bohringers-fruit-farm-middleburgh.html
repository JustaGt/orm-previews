Season by season

What's ripe, and when to come

The whole trick with pick-your-own is timing. This is the crop calendar for a normal Schoharie Valley season — call the day before to confirm the field's open, because weather moves it a week either way.

Two ways to shop

Pick your own, or let us pick

Pick-your-own

Grab a container, head out to the rows, and pick strawberries, cherries, berries or apples straight off the plant. Great with kids — a real hour outdoors, and you only pay for what you fill.

The farm stand

Short on time? The stand has ready-picked fruit and seasonal vegetables from our own fields — plus the local extras that go with a valley farm stand. Pull in off Route 30 and go.

Our family

The same family, the same valley, for over 50 years

Bohringer's has grown fruit on the floor of the Schoharie Valley for more than half a century. Generations of the same family have worked these fields — which is why the strawberries in June and the apples in October taste like a place, not a supermarket.

Come out, pick a container, and make it part of your summer the way local families have for decades.
