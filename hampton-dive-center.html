Your path from beginner to diver

It's closer than you think

You don't need to be an athlete or own any gear. You need a pool, an instructor, and an afternoon. Here's the road most of our divers take.

1

Try Scuba

Breathe underwater for the first time in a pool — no commitment, just to see if you love it.

2

Open Water

Your first real certification — dive anywhere in the world to 60 ft.

3

Advanced

Deeper dives, navigation, night and wreck skills for our local waters.

4

Rescue & Pro

Rescue diver, divemaster and instructor paths when you're ready to lead.
