Worth the drive

Why the parking lot has Vermont plates

  • Fifteen minutes from Arlington and the covered bridge — the closest full meat counter to the upper Battenkill.
  • Anglers stock coolers here before a day on the river; farm families order freezer beef cuts by phone.
  • One family name over the door for more than eight decades — the third-generation kind of place supermarkets can't copy.
