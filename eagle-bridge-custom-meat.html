Booking & drop-off

From the trailer to your freezer

A phone-only shop makes this feel like a mystery. It isn't. Here's the whole flow, start to finish.

1.Call and reserve a slaughter date — the calendar fills weeks ahead in season.
2.Drop off the animal on your date; we handle it humanely, under USDA inspection.
3.Fill out your cut sheet — cuts, thickness, ground, and anything to smoke or cure.
4.Pick up wrapped, labeled, frozen packages — cut exactly as you ordered.
