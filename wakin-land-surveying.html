Which survey do you actually need?

Four jobs people call us for

Not sure which one your bank, town, or attorney is asking for? Describe what you're doing in the form below and we'll tell you.

TYPE 01

Boundary survey

We locate and mark your true property lines and corners — for fences, disputes, splitting off a lot, or just knowing what you own. Filed map available.

TYPE 02

Mortgage / closing survey

The survey your bank or title company needs before a purchase or refinance closes — structures, setbacks, easements, and encroachments shown.

TYPE 03

Topographic / site survey

Elevations, contours, and existing features for engineers, septic design, driveways, and new construction on Catskill hillside lots.

TYPE 04

Subdivision / lot split

Dividing acreage into building lots or carving out a parcel — laid out and mapped to meet town and county filing requirements.

Tell us the parcel
