The dustless difference

A refinish shouldn't mean dust in your kitchen drawers.

Traditional floor sanding sends fine wood dust through a whole house — it settles in cabinets, vents, and closets for months. Summit runs dustless sanding equipment that captures dust at the drum, before it reaches your air. You get the new floor without the month of wiping down shelves.

Certified: BONA GreenGuard waterbase finishes

J.P. is certified in the application of BONA GreenGuard waterbase polyurethane finishes and stains — low-odor, fast-curing, and safe enough that you're back in the room in days, not weeks.
