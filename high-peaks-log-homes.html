1

We shape a plan around your land, your view and how you actually live — starting from your ideas or a Timberhaven floor plan.

Why we chose Timberhaven

Logs that line up and stay put

After 20+ years building log homes, Mike picked Timberhaven for one reason: the engineered, kiln-dried logs are straighter, check less and settle less than green stock. That's the difference between a smooth raising and a season of fighting your own walls.

  • Kiln-dried, engineered logs — graded to exceed Log & Timber Homes Council standards.
  • Most complete package in the trade — backed by a written guarantee.
  • Builder-friendly — pre-cut, pre-drilled, pre-sorted and inversely stacked.
  • Name-brand standards — Andersen windows and Therma-Tru doors included.

"Less fight to get the logs to line up."

It's not marketing — it's what builders notice on site.
