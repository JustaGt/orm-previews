Who you're working with

The same family, still at the table

In 1974, Ken Barnett created and sold monuments from a pad of graph paper at his home on Hamilton Road in Elbridge. His grandson, Bill Rinaldi, grew up helping him set stones in the cemetery. When Ken retired in 1999, there wasn't anyone better to carry it on.

That's still how it works. When you come to Barnett, you're not filling out a catalog order — you're sitting with the family, working out the stone, the shape, the wording and the artwork until it's right. Then we make it, deliver it, and set it.

"A monument is the one thing that stays after everything else is done. It should be handled by people who treat it that way."
— Three generations of the Barnett family, Seneca Falls · Auburn · Elbridge
What we make

Memorials for every kind of remembering

From a simple flush marker to a full family monument in granite or bronze — and care for the stones already in the cemetery.

Upright Monuments & Slants

Traditional standing monuments and slant markers in a range of granites, shapes and finishes — with hand-set lettering and custom artwork.

Flush & Grass Markers

Flat granite and bronze markers sized to cemetery requirements, laid level with the ground.

Bronze & Specialty Memorials

Cast bronze plaques, custom-shape and etched designs, and one-of-a-kind memorials for a life that wasn't ordinary.

Cremation Memorials & Benches

Companion and cremation stones, memorial benches and pedestals — a place to sit and remember.

Mausoleums & Statues

Private mausoleums, statuary and larger commissions, engineered and installed to last generations.

Cleaning & Restoration

Bringing weathered and older family stones back to legible — cleaning, resetting and re-lettering.

Why families come back to Barnett

Handled with care, start to finish

Ordering a memorial can feel like one more heavy task at the hardest possible time. Our job is to make that part simple — and to get every detail exactly right, because there's no fixing a name spelled wrong in granite.

  • We design it with you — wording, dates, symbols and artwork, checked and re-checked before we cut.
  • Real granite, in front of you — walk the showroom and see finishes and colors in person.
  • We handle the cemetery — permits, foundations and setting are on us.
  • Veterans' memorials — help with markers and honoring those who served.
  • We're still here after — a local family you can reach, not a website in another state.
