Repair or replace?

A straight answer before you spend anything

Plenty of outfits quote a new door for every problem. After four decades on Genesee County driveways, we'd rather tell you the truth — some doors need a $200 fix, some are rotting from the bottom rail up and deserve retirement.

Worth fixing

Sound panels with a failed spring, cable, roller, or opener. Most of these are one-visit repairs — the door has years left in it.

Worth replacing

Rotted or rusted-through sections, doors without safety cables or modern reversing sensors, uninsulated doors on heated garages. We install quality Amarr doors and openers, sized and insulated for Western New York winters.
