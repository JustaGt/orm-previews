Grown here, for here

Raised in Lowville — not trucked in from three zones south.

Big-box flowers are often started in warmer climates and shipped north soft. Ours are grown and hardened off right on Number Four Road, so they're used to the cold the day you take them home.

Why local-grown holds up

  • ✔Started and finished in our own greenhouses in Lowville.
  • ✔Hardened off for the real North Country spring — less transplant shock.
  • ✔Picked for zone-4 hardiness, not a national catalog.
  • ✔Advice from people who actually grew the plant you're buying.
