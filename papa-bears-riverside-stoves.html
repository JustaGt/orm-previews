Why buy a stove from a showroom, not a search result?

A stove is a twenty-year purchase you live beside every winter day. Photos can't show you how a door latch feels, how a firebox is welded, or how a flame pattern actually looks.

Papa Bears is part of the We Love Fire expert network — and we're a family shop on Cortland Street, not a call center. Ask us the awkward questions: what it really costs to run, what maintenance it really needs, what we'd put in our own house.
