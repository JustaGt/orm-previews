What we turn

Balls in the material your application actually needs

Different jobs need different chemistry and hardness. We work across four material families and finish to the diameter and tolerance you specify.

Thermoset Phenolic

Including hydraulic-fracturing "frac balls" — dimensioned to drop, seat and hold pressure. Chemical- and heat-resistant, dense, machinable to close diameters.

Rubber & Elastomer

Compressible balls for check valves, screen decks, agitation and cleaning. Specify durometer and diameter and we'll match it.

Metal

Steel, stainless and other metal balls for valves, bearings-adjacent uses, mill and grinding media, and weighting.

Plastic & Composite

Engineered plastics and composites where weight, corrosion resistance or non-marking surfaces matter. Plus rods, tubes and sheet stock.
