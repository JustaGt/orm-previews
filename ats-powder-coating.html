Powder vs. paint

Why powder holds when paint lets go

Liquid paint sits on the surface and chips. Powder is electrostatically applied and oven-cured into a single hard shell that bonds to the metal — the difference between a finish that lasts a season and one that lasts years.

  • Oven-cured to a hard, even shell — no runs or brush marks
  • Far more resistant to chips, scratches and corrosion
  • Holds color against UV, salt and weather
  • Hundreds of colors and textures, gloss to matte
  • No solvents — a cleaner finish, inside and out
