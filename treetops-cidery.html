The cidery

A family operation on the bluff.

Treetops sits on the high, winding East Bluff between Keuka's two arms — the kind of place you find on purpose, not by accident. The cider is made here, in small batches, by the family that planted the idea.

It's a slower stop than the big wineries: a few tables, a good pour, and one of the better views on the lake. Come up, settle in, and stay a while.

Made on the bluff · poured with the view · open to the season.

Getting up the bluff

We're on East Bluff Drive, the ridge road above Keuka. It's a climb with curves — take it easy and enjoy the lake on the way up. Parking is on-site.
