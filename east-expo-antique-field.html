For dealers

Frontage is the whole argument

Every field in Bouckville has dealers. Not every field has traffic walking past your table. What we sell is position: the road, the parking, and a layout where a shopper doesn't have to commit to a hike to reach you.

Most road frontage available

A thousand feet along Route 20 — the road every single person on the show drives down. Your booth is seen from a car before it's seen on foot.

Two rows, not a maze

Our two rows of dealers sit close to the customer parking area. Shoppers reach the far end and still have energy to buy.

Buyers who can carry it out

Furniture and heavy pieces move on a field where a car can get near the booth. That's the difference between an admirer and a sale.

Set up once, sell all week

The field is open Sunday through Sunday, with an Early Bird buyer afternoon opening the week. One setup, a full week of selling.

Food on your own field

Chicken BBQ, pork BBQ and steak sandwiches on the field all week — so your customers stay put instead of leaving to eat.

You deal with Mark

Not a booking portal, not a committee. One person who knows which spaces are actually good and will tell you.
