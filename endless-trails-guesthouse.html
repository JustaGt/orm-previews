Good to know before you haul

Equestrian riding on the Brookfield system runs May 1–Oct 31. Out-of-state horses need a valid health certificate issued within 30 days. Off-season, the property still hosts hunters, snowmobilers and cross-country skiers using the same trails.

Right out the door

The Brookfield Trail System

Over 130 miles of groomed trails across roughly 9,400 acres of state forest in southeast Madison County, maintained by the NYS DEC — the largest dedicated horse-trail network in the region. One basecamp, days of riding.
