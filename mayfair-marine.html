Tie up. Walk in. Albion's right there.

Boaters used to skip this stretch for fear of running dry — which meant they skipped Albion too. The village's downtown, with its sandstone courthouse square and canal-town main street, is a short walk from the dock. Fuel first, then go see why the county seat is worth an hour of your float plan. Four to five hundred boats pass this point every season; the smart ones now have a reason to slow down.
