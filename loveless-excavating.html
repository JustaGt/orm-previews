Septic done to code

On a septic system? Get it done by a registered installer.

Homes and cottages around Cato and Cross Lake run on their own septic — no municipal sewer. In Cayuga County, septic installs and repairs have to be done by a contractor permitted through the County Health Department. A failed system won't wait, and a bad fix costs twice.
