The story

Older than most of the sweaters it started

Very few yarn shops anywhere make it to fifty years. Homespun did it the slow way: one owner for four decades, stock chosen by hand, and generations of Ithaca-area knitters who learned that if Julie carries it, it's good fiber.

When the shop moved from the Commons to Trumansburg, the regulars followed. The address changed; the shop didn't.
