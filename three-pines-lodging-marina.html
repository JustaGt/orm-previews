The Marina Side

Everything a fishing trip needs, on the property.

The Season

Open from the walleye opener until the ice fishermen go home.

We open the first weekend of May for the start of walleye season and stay with the lake through ice-fishing season.

May — The Opener

Walleye season kicks off the first Saturday of May. Cabins 4 & 5 book fast for opener weekend — reserve early.

Summer — Family Weeks

Bass, perch, swimming off the shore, evenings at the fire pit, and boat runs to the Sylvan Beach midway.

Winter — Hard Water

When Oneida freezes, the ice-fishing crowd moves in. Ask about winter cabin availability.

Where You Are
