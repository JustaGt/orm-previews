What rolls into the shop

Work trucks, not show cars

Rust doesn't care that the truck still runs. When the box is pitted, the frame is scaling and the paint is chalking off, TEPCO takes it down to clean steel and builds the finish back up — so the unit earns another decade instead of getting traded at a loss.

Dump bodies & plow gear

Beds, hoists, wings and plow frames blasted out of the salt damage and recoated before they eat through.

Trailers & equipment

Flatbeds, tanks, excavators, loaders, farm equipment — repainted in the color and number scheme you actually run.

Fleet color matching

Running six trucks that should all be the same shade? We match to the fleet, not to a chart on a wall.

Fab & repair first

Cracked cross-members, rotted floors, broken brackets get welded and fabricated in-house before the primer coat goes on.

Our process

Four stages, no shortcuts in between

Strip & blast

Old coating, rust and scale come off. Bare, profiled steel is the only surface worth painting.

Repair & fabricate

Welding, patch panels, brackets, floors — the metalwork is fixed before it's hidden under paint.

Prime

Primer that suits the unit and the abuse it takes — road salt, gravel, hydraulic fluid, sun.

Topcoat & reassemble

Finish coat, decals and lights back on, and the truck leaves ready to work Monday morning.

Why owners bring trucks here

A new dump truck is a six-figure decision. A blast-and-repaint on a sound frame is a fraction of that, and it buys years.
