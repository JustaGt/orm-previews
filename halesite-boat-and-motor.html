What we do

A working engine shop on the harbor — not a dealership service desk. If it runs your boat, we diagnose it, fix it, or replace it.

Inboard/Outboard & Volvo Penta repair

Hard-starting, overheating, drive and outdrive problems, no-power complaints. We track down the actual fault instead of throwing parts at it.

Engine replacement & repowers

When a block is done, we pull it and repower — sourcing, fitting and aligning the new engine so the boat runs right the first time out.

Engine fabrication

Brackets, mounts, exhaust and adapter work fabricated in-house — the kind of custom engine work other shops outsource and add a wait to.

What we work on

Center-consoles, cruisers and the older I/O powerboats that a lot of shops would rather not touch. Bring us the boat you actually own.
