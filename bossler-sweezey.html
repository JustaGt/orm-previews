The reconditioning process

Three steps between a dinged wheel and a right-running boat

Whether it hit a rock or just never ran right, here's what happens when your prop reaches the bench.

01

Measure

Inboard props go on the Hale MRI for computer-aided measurement — we read the actual pitch and geometry before we touch it, so we fix what's really wrong.

02

Repair & repitch

Straighten, weld cracks and missing sections, repitch and cup blades. Bent, over-pitched, wrong pitch from the factory — corrected to the number your boat needs.

03

Verify & return

Re-checked against spec, balanced, and back to you — dropped off at the shop or shipped back by USPS/FedEx anywhere in the country.
