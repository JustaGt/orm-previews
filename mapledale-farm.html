The wait

1853

Seven generations on Greene's Brook.

The Greene family has worked this ground in the Taconic Mountains for over 170 years — through the sugarhouse years at the turn of the last century, through decades of dairying, and now through the cheese house that lets the farm's milk stay home and become something with the farm's name on it.

Making cheese here isn't a pivot or a brand exercise. It's the same idea the farm has always run on: take care of the land and the herd, and sell what the valley makes best.
