Foraged mushrooms & raw honey

Wild & cultivated mushrooms

Sustainably harvested from the woods around the farm, plus cultivated favorites — fresh in season, dehydrated the rest of the year while supplies last.

Certified in safe foraging techniques by Mushroom Mountain (ID #1122) — every wild mushroom is identified by a trained forager, not a guess.

Raw honey

Raw, unfiltered honey from hives that work the same hills the garlic grows in. Limited harvest; when the year's pull is gone, it's gone until next season.
