Field notes

Give your trophy the best start

Quick things that make a real difference before your animal ever reaches the shop. When in doubt, call — better a question than a ruined cape.

  • Don't cut the throatFor a shoulder mount, avoid cutting up the brisket or throat — that's the part we need whole for the cape.
  • Cool it fastHeat is the enemy of hair and feathers. Get the animal cool quickly and out of the sun.
  • Freeze if you can't come inCan't get to Canisteo right away? Bag it and freeze it rather than let it sit.
  • Handle fish & birds gentlyKeep fins, tails, and feathers from breaking — wrap them and call for the details.
