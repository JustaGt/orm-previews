What I bring back

One bench, the whole range of repair

Antiques and outdoor teak are the heart of the work — but the same hands handle the everyday repairs and finishes a Hamptons house goes through.

i.

Antique Restoration

Heirlooms and period pieces conserved with respect for the original — joinery, veneer, finish and patina kept intact, not sanded away.

ii.

Teak & Outdoor

Salt-grayed teak tables, chairs and loungers cleaned, repaired and re-oiled so the patio set survives another season by the water.

iii.

Repair & Refinishing

Loose joints, water rings, scratches and tired finishes on the furniture you use every day — repaired and refinished to last.

iv.

Wicker Painting

Indoor and porch wicker brought back with an even, durable finish that holds up to Hamptons weather and use.

v.

Brass Polishing

Hardware, fittings and brass pieces cleaned of tarnish and sealed back to a clean, lasting shine.

vi.

In-Home Waxing

Built-ins and large pieces that can't travel — cleaned, waxed and protected right in the house.
