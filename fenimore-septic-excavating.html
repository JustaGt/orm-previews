Why north Lake George is different

Shoreline septic isn't the same as anywhere else

Homes and camps along the north lake sit on thin, rocky soil close to the water — which is exactly why septic systems here fail differently and why the county keeps a close eye on shoreline systems. Getting a perc test read right, a system sized correctly and installed to code isn't a box to check; it's what keeps the lake clean and your permit approved.

  • Perc tests and soil evals read for real Adirondack shoreline ground, not a flat suburban lot
  • Systems sized and sited for lakeshore setbacks and rocky soil
  • Time-of-sale inspections turned around before your closing date
  • One local number for the test, the install, and anything that comes up after
