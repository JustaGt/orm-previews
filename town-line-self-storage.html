Store it right

Tips from fifteen years of watching people move

Fill boxes to capacity and label every one — half-full boxes collapse in a stack.
Leave a little space along the walls and check on your unit monthly.
Cover furniture with blankets or storage fabric — heavy plastic traps moisture.
If it's worth storing, it's worth insuring — ask us about coverage options.
