About the Shop

One welder, one standard

Adams Welding & Fabrication is Tim Adams — an Alfred State graduate and AWS-certified welder who has run the shop on Main Road in Stafford for close to three decades.

That means the person who quotes your job is the person who welds it. No hand-offs, no "we'll see what the guy can do." If Tim says it'll hold, it's his name on it.

Serving farms, contractors, small manufacturers and homeowners across Genesee County.
