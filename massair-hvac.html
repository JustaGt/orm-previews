The North Shore specialty

No ducts? No problem.

A lot of Northport-area homes were built with boilers and baseboards — no duct runs at all. That used to mean window units in every room. A ductless mini-split changes that: whole-room cooling and heating from a slim wall unit, no duct demolition, one small line through the wall.

Joe sizes and installs mini-splits every week — it's the fastest-growing part of the business, and the reviews below are from exactly these jobs.

How a mini-split install goes

  1. Joe walks the house and sizes each room — free.
  2. You get a written quote. 0% financing if you want it.
  3. Install typically wraps in a day or two per zone.
  4. Cool in summer, heat in shoulder seasons — one system.
