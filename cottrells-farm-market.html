Why buy from a grower, not a garden department?

When plants are started a few miles from where they'll live, they're already used to Finger Lakes weather — no shock from a cross-country truck, no picked-over end-of-week racks. And the person ringing you up actually grew the thing and can tell you how to keep it going.

  • Grown on-site in six greenhouses — not shipped in and marked up
  • Hardened to local weather, ready for your garden
  • Real growing advice from the family that raised it
  • Fresh, unpicked-over plants coming on all spring
  • Plants and produce in one Route 96 stop
