Took one? Do this before you bring it in

A good mount starts in the field. A few simple steps protect the cape and the memory.

1

Keep it cool & dry

Get the animal cooled down fast and keep the hide out of the sun and off wet ground.

2

Don't cut the cape short

If you want a shoulder mount, cut well behind the front legs — leave extra hide rather than too little.

3

Freeze or bring it soon

Bag it and freeze it, or drop it off promptly. Sooner is always better for the cape.

4

Call before you drive out

Give Chris a call so he knows you're coming and can tell you exactly how he wants it dropped off.
