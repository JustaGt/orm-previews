How the weekend runs

Group leaders plan whole trips around this corner. Here's the itinerary regulars already know:

Friday 6pm

Pull the trailers in, check into your block of rooms, dinner and a round at the bar while the groomers work.

Saturday 7am

Hot breakfast, fuel every sled at the on-site pump, ride out the driveway onto the trail network — no truck required.

Saturday 7pm

Back with frozen faces. Prime rib, wings, stories getting taller by the hour.

Sunday

Breakfast, top off the tanks, one last loop before the drive home.
