Why south-shore bulkheads fail

Moriches Bay shoreline takes a beating — and most failures announce themselves years ahead. Knowing the stages saves six figures.

We survey the whole run, not just the visible lean, and tell you plainly whether it's a tie-back repair or a replacement year.

Stage 1 — Soil weep.Fines washing through seams show as sinkholes behind the wall. Cheap to fix now.
Stage 2 — Tie-back fatigue.The wall bellies seaward at low tide. Anchors and walers can still save it.
Stage 3 — Toe failure.Sheathing kicks out at the mudline. Replacement window — before the upland goes with it.
