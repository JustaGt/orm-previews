Gas & cylinder services

Fills and DOT testing, close to home

TRANSFILL STATION

Medical, industrial & specialty gas fills

An FDA-registered medical-gas fill and an industrial/specialty gas station serving roughly a 60-mile radius — so clinics, labs, welders and fabricators can get bottles turned around locally.

  • Medical gas — FDA-registered fill
  • Industrial gases for welding & cutting
  • Specialty gases on request
  • ~60-mile service radius from Hornell
DOT HYDROTEST

Hydrostatic cylinder testing

A DOT-approved hydrostatic testing facility — the recertification a compressed-gas cylinder legally needs before it can be refilled. Keep your bottles in service instead of scrapping them.

  • DOT-approved hydrostatic (hydrotest) facility
  • Recertify cylinders for continued fill
  • Turnaround on local bottles
  • Ask about your cylinder type when you call
