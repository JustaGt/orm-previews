The Mones family

Three decades on the Route 281 corridor

Valley View Gardens has served Central New York for around three decades and has spent more than twenty years at 3797 Luker Road — just off NYS Route 281, opposite Builder's Best. The nursery and the Cinnamon Apple Cottage are run by the Mones family, who would rather grow you a stronger plant and point you to the right one than sell you a flat that won't make it.
