Don't wait it out

Signs a tree needs a look now

A tree that's been quietly dying is the one that comes down in the next big wind. If you're seeing any of these, it's worth a free assessment.

  • A noticeable lean that's gotten worse, especially toward the house
  • Big dead limbs, or bark falling off the trunk
  • Cracks or a split where the trunk forks
  • Roots heaving or soil lifting on one side after wind
  • Limbs touching the roof, the service line, or the driveway
  • Storm damage you're not sure is safe to leave
