Our family

A female-run ranch, raising bison the right way since 2011.

We're a family that believes good food and good land go together. The herd is 100% grass-fed and finished on rotational pasture, and we're proud to share both the meat and the story with our neighbors.

Beyond the freezer, the ranch makes bison tallow skin care, a bison wellness line, and features original bison artwork by Dayna Veazey — all available in the store and online.
