Questions we get every March

Before you call

How cold is the water, really?

It's snowmelt — 40s°F early season. That's exactly why we put you in wetsuits and why the rapids are worth it. By May it's friendlier.

Do I need whitewater experience?

No. Guides handle the reading; you handle paddling when they call it. The 6-mile lower run is the gentler introduction.

Why is the season so short?

The Cattaraugus is a free-flowing creek — no dam releases. Only spring melt makes it big enough to raft. That's the trade: a wilder river, a shorter window.

Can you take groups?

Yes — bachelor parties, teams, clubs and scout troops are our bread and butter. Tell us your headcount and we'll build the day around it.
