What comes out of the sugarhouse

Four things, all from the same trees

Everything below starts as sap from our own woods and never leaves the farm until it's finished. Sizes and prices confirmed by phone — supply runs by the season.

Syrup

Pint · Quart · Gallon

Pure Argyle maple in glass and jugs. Grade A, from Golden delicate all the way to Very Dark strong — your pick.

Sugar

Granulated maple

Pourable maple sugar for coffee, oatmeal and baking. A shelf-stable way to keep the farm on your counter all year.

Candy

Molded maple sugar

Pressed leaf and shape candy — the giveaway gift box that disappears fastest at the holiday table.

Cream

Soft maple cream

Whipped, spreadable maple with nothing added. Goes on toast, biscuits and, honestly, a spoon.

Pick by taste, not by guesswork

The four grades, plain English

Every jug of ours is Grade A. The difference is how far into the season it was made and how strong you like it. Not sure? Tell us how you'll use it and we'll match you.

Golden, Delicate

Early-season, light. Over vanilla ice cream or plain yogurt.

Amber, Rich

The classic pancake syrup most folks reach for.

Dark, Robust

Stronger maple. Great for baking and coffee.

Very Dark, Strong

Late-run, bold. For marinades, beans and BBQ.

Why buy it here

You're buying from the person who made it

Highland Maple is a working sugarbush, not a label on a store shelf. Dave taps the trees, tends the fire, and fills the jug you take home. When the sap runs during Maple Weekend, the evaporator is going and you're welcome to watch it turn from sap to syrup.

Prefer to stock it? We sell syrup by the drum to bakeries, farm stores and other makers who want a real Washington County source — ask about bulk.
