The waterfront

Two aluminum-framed, cedar-inlay docks make bringing your own boat easy — the NY State launch is nearly straight across the lake. No boat? Take out the five-person paddle boat or the two kayaks, both included, life jackets too. The floating dock sits over four and a half feet of water and is the first place the kids will swim to; its solar corner lights glow after dark.

Anglers: locals say the water off this shore is some of the best on a lake known for walleye, chain pickerel, and both bass. In summer you'll see boats parked out front — in January, ice tents.

Four honest seasons

Summer

Swim off the floating dock, paddle the shoreline, lifeguarded sand at Sandy Bottom Park near town, bonfires at the pit.

Fall

Bristol Hills color across the water; chairlift rides and zipline canopy tours at Bristol Mountain minutes away.

Winter

Baseboard-heated and fully insulated. Ice fishing villages, ice boats, XC skiing at Harriet Hollister — and Hunt Hollow's slopes visible from the shore.

Spring

Walleye season opens, herons return to the inlet marsh, and the lake is at its stillest before Memorial Day.

Good to know

Is the owner on the property?

Yes — the family cottage (built 2020) shares the acre, and renters have full use of the lawn, docks, and waterfront. It also means questions get answered fast.

Can I rent a powerboat nearby?

Trident Marine on East Lake Road rents by the hour or week, and the state launch is directly across the lake for your own trailer.

Is winter really worth it?

Between the ice-fishing villages, ice boats, and XC trails — and a warm, rebuilt cottage to come back to — winter weeks here book for a reason.
