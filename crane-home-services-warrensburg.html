For owners who aren't here

The camp you love is empty forty weeks a year

That's the honest math on most of the properties between Warrensburg, Chestertown and the lakes. Everything that goes wrong, goes wrong while nobody is looking.

A pipe doesn't announce itself. A gutter fills, backs up under the shingles and rots the sheathing over two winters. Snow builds on a low roof. A downed limb sits on the deck rail until spring, when it's a deck rail replacement instead of a five-minute job.

You don't need someone up here every week. You need someone up here at the right times, who tells you the truth about what they found.

That's the arrangement we run for absentee owners: scheduled checks, honest photos, and one call before anything gets spent. If it's fine, we tell you it's fine.
