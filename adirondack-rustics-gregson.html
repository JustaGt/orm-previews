A style he helped define, still made by hand

Barry Gregson began bending rustic furniture on Charley Hill more than forty years ago and never moved it to a factory. His signature chair — the shape people drive up the mountain to sit in — is distinctive enough to carry a U.S. patent.

Every piece is worked from local hardwood by Barry and his family, from the first cut to the finish. Nothing is ordered from a catalog and drop-shipped with his name on it.
