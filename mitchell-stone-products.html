The reclamation story

These mines built the 20th century. Now they build roads.

Benson Mine and Tahawus were working iron operations from the 1940s until the 1980s — Tahawus fed wartime steel, Benson was once among the largest open-pit magnetite mines anywhere.

The rock left behind is dense, clean and already out of the ground. Mitchell Stone Products crushes and screens that stock into aggregate — putting decades of Adirondack mining history back to work instead of opening new ground.

  • 1940sBoth mines in full production — rail lines run to each site.
  • 1980sMining ends; millions of tons of processed rock remain stockpiled.
  • TodayMSP crushes, screens and sells that rock as 100% recycled aggregate — by the ton or by the railcar.
