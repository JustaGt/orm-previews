What we build

Fence that fits the property, not a catalog

Grape-belt acreage, a lakefront lot, a horse paddock, and a commercial yard all need different fence. Here's what we install most around Chautauqua County.

Privacy & wood

Solid-board, board-on-board, and cedar privacy fence for back yards, lake homes, and pool enclosures — built to handle lake-effect winters.

Chain-link & security

Galvanized and vinyl-coated chain-link for yards, dog runs, and commercial sites that need a secure, low-maintenance line.

Farm & field line

High-tensile, woven-wire, and post-and-rail for pasture, livestock containment, vineyards, and property lines across acreage.

Ornamental aluminum

Powder-coated aluminum and decorative panels for front yards and entrances where the fence is part of the curb appeal.

Gates & access

Walk gates, double-drive gates, and cantilever openings sized to your equipment, driveway, or paddock.

Repairs & replacement

Storm-damaged sections, leaning posts, and tired old fence pulled, reset, or replaced — no full teardown required.
