WE OWN THE EQUIPMENT

The pile-driving barge is why the dock stays put

In 2008 Nick commissioned a custom pile-driving barge. That's not a detail — it's the whole difference. On deep, steep-shored Seneca Lake, a dock has to sit on pilings driven into the lakebed. Owning the barge means we schedule your job on our time, not a subcontractor's, and set pilings that survive the ice.

It also lets us haul equipment and material to shoreline sites other crews can't reach by road.

How a Dock Build Works

From shoreline visit to boat in the water

Most people have never had a dock built and don't know what to expect. Here's the honest order of it — and we keep an eye on the dock rules so your build is done by the book.

Shoreline Visit

We look at your depth, bank and how you use the water, and talk through dock, lift and wall options.

Plan & Permits

A layout built to your site — with the Army Corps / lake dock rules kept in mind so there are no surprises.

Drive & Build

The barge sets driven pilings; decking, lift and any wall work go in.

Season After Season

We're local for service, repairs and adjustments as the lake and your needs change.
