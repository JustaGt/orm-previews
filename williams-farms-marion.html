Why the muck matters

The fields east of Marion sit on drained glacial-lake bed — black, moist, fertile muck that grows the firm, clean onions and potatoes buyers come to Wayne County for. Williams Farms has worked this ground for three generations.

Growing and packing under one roof isn't common. It's the reason a buyer can point to the field the crop came from and still hand us the pack spec.
