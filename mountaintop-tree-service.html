Why spikeless

We climb on ropes, not spikes

Climbing spikes punch holes through living bark on every step — dozens of wounds that invite rot and pests into a healthy tree. That's fine on a removal. On a tree you're keeping, it's damage you paid for.

  • Rope-and-saddle climbing on every pruning job
  • Spikes reserved for removals only
  • Controlled rigging over roofs, lines and wells

The mountaintop is different

Steep access, soft shoulders, trees twice the size of anything suburban crews see, and weather that loads limbs with ice for weeks at a time.

This is the only ground we work. We know which side of the notch the wind comes from.
