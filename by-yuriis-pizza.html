Meet Yurii

From Kolomyia to Junction City

Yurii grew up in Kolomyia, in Western Ukraine. At 15 he was picked as one of three assistants to a land and zoning commissioner, which is where his interest in city planning and local government started.

At 16 he went to culinary school in Odesa, focusing on Italian and French cooking, and he came to the United States at 18. By 19 he was studying at the Institute of Culinary Education, where he was recognized as a knife instructor and took advanced training in French and Italian cuisine. He also did a four month internship in Paris.

In 2016 he cooked at Saison, a two star Michelin restaurant. By 2017 he was Executive Sous Chef for the Northeast region, overseeing operations across seven states and more than 120 restaurants and pizza places.

In 2020 he moved to Fort Riley and started cooking from home for the military community. Word got around Junction City and the towns nearby, and By Yurii’s grew from there. The dough is homemade and so is the sauce.

He gives back, too. He has funded and organized community dinners for people in need during the Christmas season, and the most recent one served 190 meals at the American Legion. He serves with the Chamber of Commerce and was recently selected for the local Housing Authority.
