Catering & events

Wood fired pizza, only at your event

Our wood fired pizza isn’t on the everyday menu. We save it for catering and private events, so your guests get pizza baked fresh right in front of them.

  • Fruit platters

Great for birthdays, weddings, corporate events and private parties around Junction City, Fort Riley, Manhattan, Ogden and Milford.
