CUT TO ORDER

CUSTOM CUTTING & PROCESSING

Bring us your animal — we'll cut it your way

Deer in season and farm livestock year-round. Tell us how your family eats and we'll wrap it that way: roasts, chops, ground, sausage and smoked.

1

Call first

Ring the counter to set up a drop-off and ask about deer-season timing before you load up.

2

Tell us the cuts

Steaks vs. roasts, grind ratio, how much sausage, what to smoke — we cut to your list, not a default.

3

We process & wrap

Cut, ground, stuffed and labeled, ready for your freezer in clean butcher wrap.

4

Pick up & stock up

Grab your order and add jerky, sticks and smoked sausage from the case on the way out.
