Cato roots

Roughly 85 years of sawdust on Cooper St

Baldwin Lumber has milled and sold to this corner of Cayuga County for generations — long enough that the barns, decks and repairs it supplied are all over Cato, Weedsport and the lake towns. That history isn't on a slick website; it's in the yard, the mill, and the people who keep coming back.

This page is a starting point for putting that yard where local builders can actually find it — hours, stock, and a way to call ahead before the drive.
