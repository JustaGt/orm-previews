Our story

Four generations on the same Camden land

The Will family first settled this land in the 1800s. In 1955, Bob and Verda Will opened it to the public. When they retired in 1996 the farm went quiet — until their granddaughter Misty and her husband Chris brought it back to life in 2011. Today it's a fall tradition for families all across Oneida County and beyond.
