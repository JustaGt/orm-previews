Four generations

Same family, same river view

Bechaz Dairy has worked this ground in Clayton for four generations. The herd grazes pastures that look out over the St. Lawrence, and that milk goes straight into the cheese room — nothing trucked in, nothing watered down.

"Creamline milk, like the milkman used to deliver."

Why creamline? Because pasteurized-but-not-homogenized milk lets the cream rise the way it did before everything got blended smooth. Why gelato? Because churning whole milk slow and dense makes a richer, lighter treat than ordinary ice cream. Small choices, four generations deep.
