Field care: the mount is won in the first hour

Bring us a well-handled cape and the finished mount will show it. Four rules before you reach the truck:

Don't cut the throat or brisket

Field dress from the sternum back. Every forward cut is a repair in the finished cape.

Leave more cape than you think

Skin well behind the front shoulders — we can always trim, we can't add.

Cool it fast, keep it dry

Heat and moisture slip hair. Get the hide cooled or frozen the same day; never store in a sealed plastic bag unfrozen.
