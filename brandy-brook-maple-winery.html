How we got here

A sugarbush that learned to make wine

It started the way most North Country mornings do — steam off the evaporator and jugs of pure syrup. Somewhere along the way the family planted cold-hardy vines, learned the craft, and became the only maple winery in New York. Today the same hands tap the trees and pour the wine, and a new wine micro-warehouse and a bigger tasting room are on the way.

Everything here is small-batch and by appointment — when you visit, you're tasting with the people who made it.
