Why the pond beats a lakefront slip

Lake Ontario makes its own weather. When a northwest blow shuts down every exposed dock on the shore, North Sandy Pond stays flat — the barrier dunes take the beating so your boat doesn't. You launch when others watch whitecaps.

Where you can get to

Through the outlet: the east-shore trout and salmon grounds. Ten minutes down Route 3: the Salmon River estuary, Selkirk Shores and Pulaski's fall run. On the pond itself: perch, bass and pike water the locals have fished for generations.
