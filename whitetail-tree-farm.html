The visit

An afternoon on the farm, not a quick stop at a lot

Our family greets you at the barn, points you to the fields, and helps you get your tree home. It's the outing families come back for every year — here's what's waiting.

🌲

Choose & cut your tree

Many varieties across the fields — walk out, find yours, and cut it. Prefer a shortcut? We keep fresh pre-cut trees ready too, and we never run out.

🪚

Free shaking, drilling & baling

We shake the loose needles, drill for a straight stand, and bale the tree for the drive — all included, no upcharge.

🧒

The Tot Lot

Kids harvest their own little tree, pick a bag of ornaments and a kid-friendly stand, and take home a wrapped surprise gift. A whole experience for a wee $19.90.*

☕

Warm up & play

Refreshments at the barn, the ISpy game in the fields, Buckaroo doughnuts and Phillbur's fudge (still $3.80*). Porta-facilities on site.

From our sugarhouse

Homemade maple — order it any time of year

We take it from tap to bottle right on the farm. Syrup, cream, nuts and fluff make easy gifts and don't wait for December. Reserve what you want and we'll set it aside for pickup, or ask about shipping.

  • Pure maple syrup pint / quart / half-gallon*
  • Maple cream new this season*
  • Maple-coated nuts gift-ready*
  • Maple fluff seasonal*
