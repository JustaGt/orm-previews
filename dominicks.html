Since 1962

A Bronx table worth the trip

Dominick's has sat on Arthur Avenue for over sixty years, in the heart of the Bronx's Little Italy. Nothing about it has been modernized for the sake of it — and that's exactly why people keep coming back.

It's the kind of place you take your parents, then years later take your own kids. The recipes haven't changed. Neither has the welcome.
