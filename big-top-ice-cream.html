The ice cream man's stand

Big Top started with John Bernas — "the ice cream man" — and it's still his family running the window today. Fifty-four years in, the stand by the tracks on Corning Road has served ice cream to three generations of the same Elmira Heights families.

Some regulars have been coming for more than thirty-five years. The prices stayed neighborly, the portions stayed generous, and the flavor board still changes every week — that's the whole secret.
