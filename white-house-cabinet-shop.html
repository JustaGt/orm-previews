How a job runs

From your measurements to your walls

  1. Talk it through

    Bring a sketch, a photo, or just the problem — the corner nothing fits, the kitchen that's done being patient.

  2. Measure & quote

    We measure the space itself and quote the job in writing — species, doors, finish, and timeline.

  3. Built in Sherburne

    Your cabinets are cut, joined, and finished on Knapp Street by the crew you spoke with.

  4. Installed right

    Scribed to your walls, doors aligned, hardware set — and the shop is ten minutes away if you ever need us.
