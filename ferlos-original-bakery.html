Four generations of flour on the floor

Ferlo's was opened over a century ago by a father and his cousin, and it never left the family. Today, brothers Carmen and David Ferlo run the ovens their father ran before them — same recipes, same corner of Rome, same insistence that bread is baked, not manufactured.

Generations of Rome families have marked their lives in Ferlo's boxes: First Communion trays, Friday tomato pie, the halfmoons that ride shotgun home to college kids who moved away.
