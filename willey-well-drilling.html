What we do

One crew for the whole life of your well

Most well problems get handed off between a driller, a plumber, and a water-treatment company. Willey does all of it in-house, so the people who drilled the well are the ones who service it.

Water Well Drilling

New drilled wells for homes, farms, schools and municipal systems — sized to your water demand and the geology under your property.

Pump & Tank Service

Submersible and jet pump replacement, pressure tanks, controls and wiring. Two dedicated pump-service trucks stocked for same-visit repairs.

Well Rehabilitation

Losing flow or getting sediment? We rehab under-performing wells before you spend on a brand-new one.

Water Treatment & Filtration

Iron, sulfur, hardness and bacteria — testing and treatment matched to what's actually coming out of your well.

Geothermal & Horizontal Drilling

Closed-loop geothermal wells for heating and cooling, plus directional/horizontal drilling for tight sites.

Parts & Retail Counter

Pumps, tanks, fittings and filtration on the shelf at the Route 39 shop — so a weekend fix doesn't wait on a shipment.
