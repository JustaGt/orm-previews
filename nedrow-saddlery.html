Fit first. Fifty years of it.

An ill-fitting saddle costs more than money — it costs the horse's back and the rider's confidence. Advice at this counter comes from five decades of horses coming through Central New York: what fits, what lasts, and what isn't worth buying twice.

The cheapest tack is the piece you only had to buy once.

A half-century on Route 11

Nedrow Saddlery opened its doors in 1974. Tack shops have vanished from Central New York one by one — the big mail-order houses came and went too. The counter at 6500 South Salina Street is still here.
