Our Story

Started by a grower's problem, in 1974

Lee Shuknecht grew up on an Elba dairy farm. Working a local repair shop, he kept hearing onion growers wish for better harvesting equipment — so he designed and built a self-propelled onion harvester himself. Joan and Lee started the company in 1974.

Fifty years on, the machines have evolved, the shop has moved to a larger building with a full parts store and machine shop, and the second generation runs the floor — but it's still a family operation building onion equipment in the heart of the muck.
