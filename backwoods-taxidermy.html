WHAT COMES THROUGH THE DOOR

Mounts built for the animals you actually hunt up here

Every species is handled differently. Here's the work the studio takes, and what to expect when you bring one in.

🦌

Whitetail Shoulder Mounts

The bread and butter. Pick your pose — sneak, semi-sneak, upright, or wall pedestal — and Brian sets the eyes and ears so it looks alive, not stuffed.

🐻

Bear — Rug or Full Body

Black bear rugs with felt borders, or a full life-size for the den. Cape and claws handled with care from the skinning shed on.

🦃

Turkey & Game Birds

Strutting toms, flying ducks, grouse. Feather work is its own craft — done in-house, not shipped out.

🐟

Fish Replicas & Skin Mounts

From the St. Lawrence and Adirondack ponds — walleye, pike, bass, brookies. Replicas available so you can release and still keep the trophy.

🦊

Small Mammals

Coyote, fox, bobcat, fisher — life-size poses on natural habitat bases for the trapline and predator hunters.

💀

European Skull Mounts

Clean, whitened skull-and-antler mounts — the simpler, modern look. A budget-friendly way to honor a deer that didn't make the wall.

FROM FIELD TO WALL

How a drop-off works

First mount? Here's the whole thing, start to finish — and a quick word on caring for your animal before you get to the shop.

Field care

Don't cut the throat. Keep the cape cool and dry. Call before you skin — a bad caping cut is the one thing that can't be fixed later.

Drop off

Bring it to the Ames Road studio. Brian talks through pose options, takes a deposit, and gives you a realistic finish date in writing.

The build

Tanning, form-fitting, eye and ear set, finish work. You're welcome to check progress — this is a studio you can actually visit.

Pickup

You get a call when it's done — on or before the date promised. Mount inspected together before it leaves.
