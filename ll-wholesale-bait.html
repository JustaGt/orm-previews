What we need from you

We sell wholesale only. Getting set up is short, but there are two things we do have to see first — stated here so nobody wastes a phone call finding out.

An EIN

A federal employer identification number for the business.

A fishing-industry business

Retail tackle, party or charter operation, commercial fishing, or an institutional aquarium or zoo.

A delivery point

The marina, dock or address the order goes to, and the day that suits you.

No public sales

We do not sell to individual anglers — that is what keeps our retailers' margins intact.
