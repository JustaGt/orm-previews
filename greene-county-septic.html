What we handle

Six services, one phone number that actually dials.

Homes on septic, restaurants with grease traps, and job sites that need a clean porta-john — all served off the same Route 9W yard, most calls same or next day.

1

Septic Tank Pumping

Full pump-out and inspection of the tank, baffles and lid. We tell you what we see so a slow drain doesn't turn into a backup in the basement.

2

Electric Sewer Cleaning

Powered cable to cut roots and clear a clogged main line when the toilet won't go down and the yard's fine.

3

Water Jetting

High-pressure jetting to flush greasy or sludged lines clean — not just poke a hole through the blockage.

4

Grease Trap Service

Scheduled pumping and cleaning for kitchens and taverns, with the hauling paperwork your health inspector wants to see.

5

Portable Toilets

Sludge Hauling
