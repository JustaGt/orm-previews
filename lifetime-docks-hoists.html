Why everything we build is aluminum

Wood rots and steel rusts — both are a losing battle at a waterline that's wet six months and frozen the other six. Our docks, lifts, stairs and trailers are welded from high-grade aluminum by trained welders, so they go in light and come out of the lake the same shape they went in.

Light enough to pull and store before ice-up, strong enough to leave a lift loaded all summer.
