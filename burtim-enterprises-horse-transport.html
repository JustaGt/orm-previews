You're handing over a valuable, living animal. Here's exactly what that looks like with us.

1

Loading

Patient, no-rush loading — box stall or straight load depending on your horse and the trip.

2

On the road

Steady driving, good ventilation, hay and water, and regular stops to check and offer water on long runs.

3

Communication

You get pickup confirmation and updates in transit, so you always know where your horse is.

4

Arrival

Calm unloading and hand-off at the destination barn, with time to settle before we leave.
