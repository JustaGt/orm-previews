Our story

Same family, same greenhouse, going on 40 years

C & J Farms has been growing plants and flowers on Lower Road in Constantia for about four decades — right on the north shore of Oneida Lake. What started as a family greenhouse is still run the same way: we grow it, we tend it, and we're here when you come to pick it out.

That's the difference from a big-box garden section. The baskets aren't a shipment — they're this season's crop, raised a few feet from where you'll buy them.
