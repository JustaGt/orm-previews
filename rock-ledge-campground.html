The park sits on one of Oneida County's prettiest waterways — the same cool, clear creek that eventually feeds Oneida Lake.

The comforts that matter

A small park done properly beats a big park done loudly.

  • Tiled bathrooms with handicapped-accessible showers — kept clean, not just kept.
  • Laundry on site so a week of creek and campfire doesn't come home in a bag.
  • Playground where kids burn the last of the day's energy.
  • Quiet, friendly park — seasonal regulars who wave, not wall-to-wall party sites.
