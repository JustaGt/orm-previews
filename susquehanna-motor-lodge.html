Before you drive out

The things people call and ask

Can I bring the dog?

Yes — pets are welcome. Mention it when you book so we put you in the right spot.

Do you take tents?

No. The campground is RV-only. Rooms are the option if you're not towing.

Will my rig fit?

Sites take up to about 70 feet, with 8 pull-thrus. Call with your length and we'll tell you honestly.

How do I book?

Phone. (607) 967-2102. Booking direct means no third-party fee and you can ask for the room you liked last time.
