What we do

Ground work, start to finish

One crew for the whole job — clear it, dig it, set the base, and bring the material. Here's the work we're set up for:

Excavation & Digs

Foundation and full-basement excavation, footings, utility trenching, and grading — dug to plan and backfilled clean.

Driveways & Access Roads

New driveways, long farm/camp access roads, culverts, and regrading of washed-out lanes. Built for mud season and frost.

Sand, Gravel & Fill

Bank-run gravel, crushed stone, sand, topsoil and fill — supplied and delivered from our own material.

Site Prep & Land Clearing

Clearing, stumping, brush and stripping for new builds, additions, and farm expansions.

Drainage & Ditching

Ditches, ponds, culverts, curtain drains and regrading to move water away from buildings and fields.

Trucking & Hauling

DOT-authorized trucking to move material in or spoil out — dump-truck and equipment hauling.
