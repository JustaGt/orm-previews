Who we are

We're a co-op, not a franchise. The dancers run the room.

The New Paltz Dance Coop grew out of weekly tango nights at Roost Studios on Main Street — a handful of regulars who wanted somewhere unpretentious to learn and practice. It still feels that way.

You'll find seasoned leads and followers happy to dance with newcomers, instructors who teach the why and not just the steps, and socials where nobody minds if you count out loud. Singles welcome — we rotate partners.

Walk in nervous, leave with a new favorite song.
