About the Practice

An older medicine, attentively practiced

Chinese medicine reads the body as a whole — sleep, digestion, tension, the quality of your pulse. Zhaohui Meng spends the first visit understanding that picture before treatment begins.

Needles are fine and placed with care; herbal formulas are tailored to you, not pulled off a shelf. The aim is steady, durable improvement, not a quick patch.
