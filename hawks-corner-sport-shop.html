Buying online? Here's how the transfer works.

New York adds steps most out-of-state sellers don't explain. We do this every week — here's the whole path, so nothing surprises you.

1

Have the seller ship to us

Give the retailer or auction house our FFL details — we'll fax or email a copy of the license on request.

2

We log it in

Your firearm is checked in and secured the day it arrives. We call you — you're never chasing a tracking number.

3

Paperwork at the counter

Background check and NY-required steps done here. Bring your ID (and your permit for handguns).

4

Walk out right

We hand it over only when everything is lawful and complete — and we'll bore-sight a new scope while you're here if you'd like.
