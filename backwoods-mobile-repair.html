The no-tow math

What a "cheap" shop visit actually costs

A flatbed to Buffalo, three days in a shop queue, and a ride back — before anyone touches a wrench. If it's a combine, a skid steer, or a loaded trailer, "bring it in" isn't even an option. Mobile repair flips the math: one visit, on your ground, at in-shop rates.
