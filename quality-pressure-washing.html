Why it matters

Soft wash vs. pressure — and why your roof needs the gentle one

Soft Wash (low pressure)

A cleaning solution does the work, then rinses clean. Safe for the surfaces a high-pressure blast would damage.

  • Roof shingles & ridge caps
  • Vinyl, wood & stucco siding
  • Screens, soffits and trim
  • Kills algae at the root so it stays gone longer

Pressure Wash (high pressure)

Real water pressure for hard surfaces that can take it — where you want grime physically scrubbed off.

  • Concrete driveways & sidewalks
  • Brick & paver patios
  • Block foundations
  • Dumpster pads & commercial flatwork
