The Mill

One band saw, one sawyer

Capwell's is a real working mill on Richley Road in Corfu — Tony Capwell running a Type-1 single-band saw, turning local logs into usable lumber for Genesee County woodworkers and builders.

It's the kind of place that lives on repeat customers and word of mouth. What it's never had is a spot online where a new woodworker can see the species, understand custom milling, and get a phone number that isn't buried in a state directory. That's what this page fixes.
