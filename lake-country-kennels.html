Before you book

What to have ready at drop-off

A few simple things keep every pet in our care safe and healthy. Knowing them up front saves a scramble on the day you leave.

  • Current rabies and bordetella (kennel cough) records at arrival
  • Your dog on a leash when you pull in, for everyone's safety
  • Tell us about any medications before booking so we can plan for them
  • We are not able to accept aggressive dogs
