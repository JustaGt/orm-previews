The Way

Heritage you can feel

Isshin-Ryu means "one-heart way." Every class begins and ends with respect, and the techniques you learn here trace directly back to Okinawa.

Sensei Borland keeps the tradition intact: solid stances, real kata, and the kind of slow, careful instruction that builds skill that lasts. Students aren't rushed through belts — they earn each one, and they understand what it means.

What people carry out of the dojo is bigger than karate: focus, humility, and the quiet confidence that comes from disciplined practice.
