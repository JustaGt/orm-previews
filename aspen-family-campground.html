Forty sites. A hundred wooded acres. One quiet hill above Candor.

Aspen Family Campground sits 18 miles south of Ithaca — far enough that the loudest thing at night is the fire. Water & electric sites for RVs and tents, a saltwater pool, and a five-acre pond you can fish before breakfast.

Sites & hookups

Pick your spot under the trees

Water + electric RV sites

Most of our forty sites carry water and 30-amp electric, with select 50-amp sites for bigger rigs. Every site comes with a fire ring and picnic table. Tell us your rig length when you book and we'll match you to a pad that fits.

Tent sites in the woods

Tenters get real tree cover, not a mowed strip beside the RVs. Hot showers are a short walk away, and the central fire pit hosts the kind of evenings kids talk about all winter.

Two ways to get wet

A saltwater pool and a pond of your own

24,000-gallon saltwater pool

Gentler on eyes and swimsuits than chlorine — and not something you'll find at many campgrounds our size. Heated by the sun, watched by the lifeguard chair, loved by every kid on the hill.

The five-acre pond

Fish it, row it, swim it. Bass at dawn, small boats all afternoon. No motors, no wake — just water doing what water does in the woods.
