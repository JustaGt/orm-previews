Capabilities

One shop that spans the whole range

Plenty of shops pick a lane — production plate, or ornamental, or repair. Benemy runs all of it, which is why the same crew that welds a factory shred-hopper also builds a homeowner's stair rail. Here's what comes through the doors.

01

Sheet & plate metal

Precision sheet- and plate-metal fabrication for guards, hoppers, platforms, brackets and enclosures — formed, cut and welded to spec.

02

Ornamental & architectural iron

Railings, gates, gangways, stairs and custom architectural pieces — from a residential porch rail to public handrail runs and marina dock arms.

03

CNC plasma cutting

CNC plasma cutting including pipe, for clean repeatable parts, brackets and profiles cut from your file or ours.

04

Machine-shop work

Brake, mill and shear work plus machining to finish and fit fabricated parts — one stop from raw stock to a working assembly.

05

Metal supply

Need the steel, stainless or aluminum as well as the labor? We handle material supply so you're not sourcing stock from three vendors.

06

Portable welding & truck lift

Portable MIG/TIG/ARC welding for on-site repairs and installs, plus truck-lift service when the job can't come to the shop.

Who we build for

Three kinds of customer, one quote line

Industrial

Plants and processors needing hoppers, guards, sort tables, wash racks, platforms and production parts — built to keep a line running.

Ex: shred hoppers · box pushers · sort sinks

Commercial & municipal

Contractors, marinas, libraries and towns needing railings, gangways, gates, book drops and dock hardware that has to pass inspection and last.

Ex: dock arms · handrail runs · cemetery gate

Residential

Homeowners who want a real fabricator for a porch rail, custom bracket, repair or one-off — not a big-box special-order.

Ex: porch railings · trellis · sign brackets
