Two kinds of work

Fix the broken one, or build the new one

Most jobs that come through the door are one of two things: something cracked or snapped that needs to run again, or a part that doesn't exist yet and needs to be made. We do both.

Repairs

Bring in the broken part — or the whole machine. If it can be welded back to working, that's usually cheaper and faster than replacing it.

  • Cracked buckets, hitches & equipment
  • Trailer frames, ramps & tongues
  • Snapped brackets, racks & mounts
  • Aluminum & cast pieces others turned down

Custom Fabrication

Need a part made to spec? Bring a sketch, a sample, or just the problem, and we'll fabricate it to fit — one-off or a small batch.

  • Brackets, frames & stands
  • Railings, gates & guards
  • Equipment mods & add-ons
  • One-off parts you can't buy

Why "certified" matters on a weld

A weld that looks fine and a weld that holds aren't always the same thing — especially on aluminum, stainless and cast, where bad technique cracks under load. Every weld here is done by a fully certified welder.
