Three ways to put your name on a shirt

We'll tell you straight which method your job actually needs — that's the advantage of talking to the person who runs the press.

Screen Printing

Best for: 12+ pieces, bold art

Ink laid through a real screen, cured to survive a season of practices and a hundred washes. The per-shirt price drops fast as quantity climbs — ask where your order's break points are.

Embroidery

Best for: polos, caps, workwear

Stitched logos read "established business" on a chest or a cap like nothing else. We digitize your art once, then run it on anything from beanies to jackets.

Heat Transfer

Best for: names, numbers, small runs

Player names, single replacement shirts, personalization on top of printed stock. The right tool for the jobs screen printing is wrong for — and we'll say so.
