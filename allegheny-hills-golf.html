The Course

A real test, built into the hillside

Henry Bruckert carved Allegheny Hills out of the countryside near Rushford in 1960, and it still plays the way a country course should — natural water hazards, a few well-placed bunkers, and yardage you can actually read.

  • Full 18 holes, or play a quick 9 after work
  • Natural water hazards and well-placed sand bunkers
  • Easy-to-read yardage markers throughout
  • Practice putting green to warm up before the first tee
  • Walk the course or rent from a fleet of 50 carts
  • Club rental on hand if you're traveling light
