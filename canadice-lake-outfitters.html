Before you launch

Do I need to sign a waiver?

Yes — every paddler signs a rental waiver. With the new site you'd sign it online when you book, instead of on paper at the trail.

Can I fish from the boats?

Absolutely. Canadice holds trout, bass, and panfish, and the no-wake quiet makes it a favorite of kayak anglers.

Is swimming allowed?

No — Canadice is a protected water supply. Swimming and camping are prohibited, which is exactly why it stays this quiet.

What should I bring?

Lunch, water, sunscreen, and a camera. Life jackets, paddles, and dry bags are included with every rental.
