Why a lake lot is different

Steep grades, tall timber, water below

A Chautauqua Lake property isn't a flat suburban yard. The trees are old and tall, the grade drops toward the water, and a wrong cut ends up on the roof, the dock, or in the lake. That's the work we do every week around this shoreline.

  • Tall shoreline hardwoods & hemlocks on steep, wet grades
  • Takedowns that have to fall away from the roof, dock and neighbor
  • Lake-effect snow and wind that drop limbs every winter
  • Seasonal owners who need a trusted crew on the property while they're away
