What we dig

Mostly residential — new builds, camp lots and property projects around Mayfield and the lake. If it involves moving dirt or hauling it off, it's on the list.

Site clearing

Clear and rough-grade a wooded or overgrown lot so a house, garage or camp can go up on it.

Foundation excavation

Dig and prep for foundations, footings and slabs — cut to the builder's plan and backfilled clean.

Septic systems

Install and replace residential septic — tank, field and the excavation around it, for new builds and failed old systems.

Driveways

Cut, base and grade gravel driveways that hold up to lake-country winters and mud season.

Water & electric lines

Trenching and backfill for water service and underground electric out to the house.

Pond digging

Dig and shape ponds — a request the box-store contractors don't touch and lake-property owners actually want.

Grading & drainage

Final grade, swales and rough drainage so water runs away from the building, not toward it.

Trucking & hauling

Own trucks to bring in fill, gravel and stone and haul spoil off — no waiting on a subbed-out hauler.

Where we work

Based in Mayfield, right on the building belt north of Amsterdam and around the Great Sacandaga Lake — the corridor where new homes and camps keep going up.
