Most patios get planted as an afterthought. Ours are designed as one picture: beds shaped to soften walls, plantings chosen for this valley's zone and deer pressure, stone and green planned in the same drawing. That's the difference a design-build family shop makes.

About

The Becker crew, Ashgrove Road

Becker Landscape LLC is a family business based on Ashgrove Road in Cambridge with more than twenty years in hardscape and garden design. Small crew, owner on site, and a habit of being easy to reach — because in a valley this size, the last job is always the reference for the next one.

A note for searchers: we're Becker Landscape of Cambridge, NY — not the similarly named property-maintenance outfits elsewhere in the Capital Region. If you meant the folks on Ashgrove Road, you're in the right place.
