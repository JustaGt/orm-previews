One family, two kinds of fresh

The produce stand and the material yard

Most farm stands sell you dinner. We also sell you the dirt for the garden it came from. Here's both sides of B.J. Farms.

The Produce Side

Grown here, picked ripe, sold at the stand

  • Sweet corn — picked the same morning it hits the table
  • Tomatoes, peppers & summer veg — the daily haul
  • Berries & fruit — strawberries into the summer crops
  • Pick-your-own — bring the kids and fill a basket
  • Local finds — we round out the stand with other area farms' goods

The Material Side

By the scoop or by the load, delivered

  • Screened topsoil — clean fill for beds, lawns & grading
  • Washed sand & stone — for drainage, pads & projects
  • Fill dirt & gravel — bulk quantities for the bigger job
  • Delivery — tell us the town and we'll bring it
  • Wholesale — landscapers & contractors, ask about volume
