The 4:30 a.m. phone call

Same-day parlor service

Call the shop, describe what the parlor is doing — losing vacuum, slow milk-out, a unit that won't pulse, a wash cycle that won't finish — and we'll tell you on the phone whether it's a part you can swap yourself or one of ours coming out with the truck. Standing parts you shouldn't run out of
