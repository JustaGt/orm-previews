What we survey

Every kind of survey a Genesee County property needs

If you're not sure which one your situation calls for, describe it and we'll point you to the right survey — not the most expensive one.

M

Mortgage / Title Survey

The instrument survey your bank, title company, or attorney needs to close on a home or refinance. Done to meet the closing date.

B

Boundary Survey

Pins set and lines marked on the ground — for a new fence, a driveway, a shed, or to settle exactly where your property ends and the neighbor's begins.

F

Farm & Acreage Splits

Subdividing or buying farmland, carving out a building lot, or recording a deed description for a parcel of GLOW-belt acreage.

T

Topographic Survey

Elevations and existing features mapped for an architect, engineer, or builder before a project breaks ground.

C

Commercial / ALTA

Surveys for commercial purchases, lenders, and development — prepared to the standard the deal requires.

S

Construction Stakeout

Corners and offsets staked so your contractor builds the foundation, addition, or structure where it's supposed to go.

How it works

Three steps, no mystery

You'll know the turnaround and the price before any field work begins.

1. Send the request

Property address, tax-map number, why you need it, and your deadline. We confirm what survey fits and quote it.

2. Field & records work

We pull the deeds and county records, then walk the property — finding monuments, setting pins, taking the measurements.

3. The recorded map

You get a sealed, signed survey map your bank, attorney, or town will accept — delivered to meet your closing or permit date.

Who relies on us

Homeowners, attorneys, title companies & farmers

  • Homeowners — buying, refinancing, building a fence, or finally settling a boundary question.
  • Attorneys & title companies — who need a clean instrument survey to close on schedule.
  • Farmers & landowners — splitting acreage, recording a deed, or buying the next field over.
  • Builders & contractors — who need an accurate stakeout before the concrete is poured.
