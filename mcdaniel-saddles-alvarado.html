The family story

From a Panhandle repair bench to three generations of saddles

Preston McDaniel learned the craft from his father, John — a master saddle maker who repaired saddles for cowboys in the Texas Panhandle back in the 1920s. Preston built his first saddle in 1965, and the shop has grown steadily ever since, now with three generations working the leather side by side. The rule his daddy gave him still runs the shop: build the saddle to fit the horse, then the rider, make it last, and put a lifetime guarantee on it.

"First you build the saddle to fit the horse. If he's not comfortable, he's not gonna work for you. Then you build it to fit the rider and to last — and you put a lifetime guarantee on it."

— Preston McDaniel, on what his father taught him
