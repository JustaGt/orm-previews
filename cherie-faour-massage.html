About Cherie

A licensed therapist who actually listens

For years, Cherie has built a quiet practice on a simple idea: no two bodies carry stress the same way. Each appointment starts with a few questions and a plan, then unhurried, focused work.

Whether you're recovering from an injury, easing chronic tightness, or simply long overdue for rest, the pressure and pace are set by you.

— Cherie Faour, LMT
