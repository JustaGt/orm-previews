What we dig

Excavation for homes, farms, and land

Most of what we do is the work nobody sees once it's done — the septic field under the lawn, the footing under the house, the pond that holds water through August.

Septic systems

New system installs and repairs — tank, field, and grading — done to code and ready for the county inspection.

Ponds

Farm, livestock, and recreation ponds dug, shaped, and sealed where the ground allows, with proper drainage.

Foundations & footings

Driveways & culverts

Land & lot clearing

Why it matters who you hire

Septic is the one you don't want done wrong

A bad septic install is a five-figure problem you find out about the hard way. Emery Masiker is on the Chautauqua County onsite-wastewater contractor list, so the system gets put in the way the county expects — and signed off.

What a septic job includes

  • Perc/soil conditions reviewed before any digging
  • Tank set and field laid to the approved design
  • Grading and cover so the field drains and stays dry
  • Coordinated with the county inspection process
  • Repairs and replacements for failing older systems
