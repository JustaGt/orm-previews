A fourteenth-generation bayman runs this counter.

Clint Bennett has clammed and fished Montauk's bays and offshore grounds his whole life — same as the thirteen generations before him.

He pulls the catch; Kim cooks it. The clams in your bag and the clams in the pie came out of the same water, often the same morning. There's no distributor in the middle and no second location — just one small shop where the person who caught your dinner is usually the one handing it to you.

That's the whole pitch: you can taste exactly how short the trip from the boat to your bag was.
