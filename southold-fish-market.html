Who's behind the counter

Run by a fisherman, not a chain

Charlie Manwaring has fished these waters for decades and run the market for more than twenty-five years. That's the whole pitch: the person choosing the fish for your dinner is the same person who knows which boats came in and what's actually good this week. No middleman, no freezer truck — just what's fresh, sold straight.

Locals come for the weekly catch. Visitors make it the stop on their North Fork day. Either way, it's fresh, it's fast, and it tastes like the water it came out of. — The Southold Fish Market way
