A spey shop first, not an afterthought

Most shops keep a couple of two-handed rods in the corner. Here the swing is the specialty: skagit and scandi heads matched to the rod in your hand, sink tips sorted for Altmar-to-Pineville flows, and honest advice on what a first spey setup should cost — and what it shouldn't.

The tying wall

Hooks, hackle, dubbing, tube-fly hardware and every shade of egg yarn the river asks for. If you tie your own intruders, buggers, or estaz eggs, the material is on the wall — and if it isn't, mail order will find it.

Salmon, September–November

Kings and cohos fresh from the lake. Rigging, leader wallets, and the patterns working this week — tied locally, not shipped from a warehouse.

Steelhead, November–April

The long season the fly zone was made for. Swing tips, indicator setups, and cold-weather layering that actually holds up on a January drift.

Service bench

Line swaps, reel cleaning, guide repair. Bring the rod that cracked on the drive up — most fixes turn around while you fish.
