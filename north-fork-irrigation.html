The North Fork year

Time it right and nothing freezes

The East End runs on a tight seasonal clock. Here's when to have us out — reserve the spot before the rush.

Apr – May

Start-up

Turn the system on, pressure-test, fix winter damage, set the schedule for the season.

Jun – Aug

Peak & repairs

Dry stretch = heaviest run time. This is when leaks and dead zones show up — call the moment one does.

Sep – Oct

Adjust

Dial back run times as the season cools; tune drip on the fall garden and late vines.

Late Oct – Nov

Blow-out

Book the winterize before the first hard freeze. Miss it and lines crack — this is the one not to skip.
