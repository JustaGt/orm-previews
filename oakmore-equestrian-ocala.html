What we do

One program, built to win and to keep it enjoyable

Natalie and Shawn keep the same standard of horsemanship across every horse in the barn, from a green prospect to a made show mount.

Training Board

Full-care board with structured training — five days a week of rides or lessons, prepping horse and rider to compete at any level.

Sales & Leases

Sales and lease horses matched to the rider and the goal, with honest trials and a straightforward commission structure.

Young Horses

Long-term development for young and green horses in a quieter, structured setting built for steady progress.

Breeding

A breeding operation at the home farm, feeding the young-horse and sales program with prospects raised right from the start.

Two home bases, one philosophy

Aligned facilities for every stage of a horse's career

Diasti Stables — Sales & Training

A 110-acre luxury facility in Morriston with multiple arenas, riding trails and grass paddocks — the base for the sales and active-training program, minutes from HITS and WEC.

Oakmore Home Farm — Young Horses & Breeding

The quieter home farm in Ocala, about five minutes from HITS and eight from WEC — built for young-horse development and breeding in a low-stress setting.
