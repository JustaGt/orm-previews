What the lumber aisle can't do

Full-dimension stock

Rough-sawn 2× actually measures 2×. For barns, fences and farm builds, that extra wood matters — and you're not paying for kiln-dried framing you don't need.

Sizes they don't carry

Sixteen-foot hemlock, oversized timbers, odd thicknesses — the cuts a chain store would special-order for weeks come straight off the mill here.

Native & close to home

Lumber sawn from Central New York timber, a short drive up County Route 6 — not trucked across the country with a markup at every stop.
