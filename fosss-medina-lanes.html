From pin chaser to proprietor

Jim Foss, co-owner and manager, has worked at this bowling alley for about forty years — table cleaner, pin chaser, head mechanic, and now owner. He's the guy other alleys in the region call when their machines break. His name is on the 800-series wall of fame, and he still carries a 225 average.

His son Chris grew up here and bowls a 212. They bought the lanes in 2021 from owners who'd already put in synthetic lanes and modern scoring — and they kept every bit of the place that makes it feel like Medina's living room.
