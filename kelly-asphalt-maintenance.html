Why upkeep wins

What Chenango winters do to unsealed blacktop

Year 1–2

Hairline cracks open at the surface. Cheap to seal now — pennies per foot.

Year 3–5

Water gets in, freezes, and pries the cracks wide. Edges start to crumble.

Year 6–9

The base goes soft. Potholes and alligator cracking — now it's patch territory.

Year 10+

Full repave, at many times the cost of the sealing schedule that would have prevented it.

General freeze-thaw progression for unmaintained asphalt — every driveway is quoted on its actual condition.
