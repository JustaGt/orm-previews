Who we are

The shop families in Lyons keep coming back to

Roger Clark and Geoff Grambo run a shop that's served Lyons and the surrounding Wayne County villages for more than three decades — selling and setting memorials, and coming back to add a name when the time comes.

Most of our work is for people we've helped before. We answer the phone, we know the local cemeteries, and we do the work ourselves.
