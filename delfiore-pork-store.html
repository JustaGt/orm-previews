Three names on the wall, one family behind the counter

Delfiore opened in 1971 — and more than fifty years later it's still one of the last family-owned and operated pork stores on Long Island. That's not a marketing line; it's a headcount. Most of them are gone.

Come in and you'll find a member of the family working the counter, same as always. That's the difference you can taste, and the reason people who moved away still stop in when they're back on the Island.
