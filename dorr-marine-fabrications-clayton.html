What We Build

Custom metal work for the water

Not off-the-shelf kits — steel built to the job in front of us. Whether it's a dock section for a private shoreline or a push boat for a marina, the work is measured, cut and welded to spec.

Floating Docks

Custom steel-frame floating docks engineered for St. Lawrence current, ice and the depth off your shoreline.

Barges

Work barges built and repaired for hauling material, equipment and dock sections out to the islands.

Push Boats

Boat Lifts

Pontoon & Trailer Repair

Sandblasting
