The farm year

A reason to come back every season

The market changes with the fields. Here's what's worth the drive out to Kendall, month by month — so you always know what you'll find when you pull in.

April – June

Spring · Get the garden going

  • Bulk & bagged mulch, topsoil, manure, potting soil
  • Bedding plants, perennials & a full seed rack
  • Ice cream bar opens in April
June – August

Summer · Corn & cones

  • Fresh-picked sweet corn & garden vegetables
  • 32 flavors of Perry's ice cream & custard
  • The grill's on — burgers, dogs & the picnic grove
September – October

Fall · Apples & pumpkins

  • Mix-and-match apples from the bins & old-fashioned cider
  • Weekend hayrides to the pick-your-own pumpkin patch
  • Fall vegetables, baked goods & the full gift shop
November – December

Holidays · Trees & wreaths

  • Fresh Christmas trees, wreaths & centerpieces
  • Jams, honey, pantry mixes & Cheesy Eddie's cakes
  • Gift & décor ideas all through the shop

The Partyka family

Five generations in, and the sixth is in the fields

Jeff and Lora Partyka come from five generations of Western New York farmers. Their sons Scott and Steve — the sixth generation — are taking on more of the farm every year. They grow, harvest, and roast right here, and keep the market manageable on purpose: so every visitor still gets the finest product and a personal hello.

"Our farm has become a place for people to visit. We like being part of the community and seeing the families and ball teams enjoying themselves." — Lora Partyka
