What we do

Five jobs. Done right, in the right order.

Sealcoating

Deep-black protective coat that shields asphalt from UV, water and road salt — the single cheapest year you'll ever add to a driveway's life.

Hot & Cold Crack Repair

Cracks are filled and sealed before any coating goes down, so water can't get under the surface and freeze it apart.

Line Striping

Sharp, straight parking stalls, arrows and ADA symbols for churches, stores, clinics and town lots around Malone.

Pothole Repair

Oil-Spot Treatment

Oil bleed-through ruins fresh sealer. Spots get primed and treated first so the finish stays uniform and black.

Why seal here, of all places

Freeze-thaw is the enemy.
Malone gets a lot of it.

Every time water slips into a hairline crack and freezes, it pries the crack wider. The North Country cycles above and below freezing dozens of times each spring and fall — then the plow blade finds every raised edge.

Sealcoating closes the surface before winter gets its fingers in. A driveway sealed on schedule costs a fraction of the repaving job an unsealed one eventually demands.

How a job goes

Clean. Repair. Seal. Stripe.

Clean & Edge

Power broom and blow-off; grass edged back so sealer reaches the true edge of the asphalt.

Repair First

Cracks hot-filled, potholes cut and patched, oil spots primed. Sealer over bad repairs is wasted money.

Seal

Commercial-grade sealer applied by squeegee or spray depending on surface age and texture.

Stripe & Cure

Lots get fresh layout lines and ADA marking. Cones stay up until the surface is ready for tires.
