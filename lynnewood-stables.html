What we do

Four ways to ride with us

A small, hands-on program — which means Mike knows every horse and every rider by name, and builds each week around where you actually are.

01

Lessons

Private and semi-private instruction for juniors and adult amateurs, from the crossrails up. Confidence first, then results.

02

Training

Full training for your horse — flatwork, gymnastics, and ring miles — so you both show up to the show ready.

03

Showing

Coaching at rated shows across the Northeast and the winter circuit in Wellington. We school, warm up, and stand at the ring with you.

04

Sales

Finding and vetting the right horse — a first pony, an equitation mount, or a step-up jumper — and matching it to the rider.

The trainer

"I concentrate on building a solid foundation by instilling confidence in both horse and rider — always putting the needs of the horse before anything else."

Michael Zukerman · Owner & Head Trainer

Mike grew up showing on Long Island in the Junior Hunter, Junior Jumper, and Equitation divisions, then turned professional and founded Lynnewood Stables — named for his favorite equitation horse, Lynnewood Hall. He's had top placings from the High Performance Working Hunters and Young Jumpers to the Grand Prix ring, and his students have won from Short Stirrup all the way up. He, his wife Lexey Hall, and their children Zachary and Lucy call Long Island's North Shore home.

The facility

Home base: Hunter's Moon Farm

A 60-acre farm in East Norwich, minutes from Old Westbury and Muttontown.

  • Newly renovated barns with roomy, well-bedded stalls
  • Spacious outdoor rings with state-of-the-art footing
  • A new indoor arena for year-round riding
  • Large grass paddocks plus all-weather sand turnout
  • Direct access to the Muttontown Preserve — 550 acres of trails
  • Winter base in Wellington, FL for the circuit
