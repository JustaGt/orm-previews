Know your farmer

A farm you can actually drive to

The whole point of Heritage Acres is that it isn't anonymous. The beef and pork come from animals raised right here in Delanson, on pasture, without hormones or antibiotics — and you pick it up from the people who did the raising.

That's something a grocery-store label can't give you: the address, the fields, and a phone number that reaches the farmer. If you've been looking for meat you can trace to a real place, this is the place.
