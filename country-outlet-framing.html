What comes through our door

If it matters to you, it fits in a frame

Family photos & portraits

From wallet prints to wall-size enlargements — matted so they never touch the glass.

Needlework & textiles

Cross-stitch, quilts, christening gowns — stretched and mounted without a single adhesive touching the fabric.

Shadow boxes

Military medals, arrowheads, a first dollar bill, baby shoes — built with depth to hold the real thing.

Canvas & artwork

Oil, acrylic, watercolor or print — stretcher bars, float frames, conservation glass when the piece calls for it.

Jerseys & memorabilia

Game jerseys, signed pucks and photos, race bibs — displayed, not folded in a drawer.

Diplomas & documents

Degrees, discharge papers, marriage certificates — acid-free backing so the paper outlives us all.
