What we build & repair

Two things happen in this shop: we keep working equipment running, and we design the one-off machines growers can't find anywhere else.

Custom fabrication

Frames, guards, hitches, platforms, brackets and rigs designed and built to a grower's exact rows, tractor and crop — not adapted from a generic part.

Machine shop

Turning, boring, shafts, bushings and precision metal work to bring worn or discontinued parts back to spec.

Repair & rebuild

Welding and structural repair on sprayers, wagons, bins, trailers and orchard equipment that's still good iron under the wear.

Design work

Have an idea for a job that's always been done by hand? Bring the problem — we've turned grower sketches into working, patent-pending machines.

Designed & built in Peru

The mechanized pruning platform

Orchard pruning and thinning has always meant crews on ladders. Richard and Nick designed a self-propelled work platform — pneumatic pruning guns, electric assist and room for a crew to move down the rows at height, safely and far faster than by hand. The design is distinct enough that a patent is pending.
