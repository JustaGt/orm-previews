Why it matters on Keuka

Ice-out is the real test of a dock.

A dock that floats through summer can still be shoved, heaved, or snapped when Keuka freezes and lets go in spring. The pilings, the wall footing, and where the ice-eater sits are what decide whether you're rebuilding next May.

We build for the freeze first — then the sunny July afternoon takes care of itself.

  • Pilings driven to holding depth for your section of shore
  • Seawall footings set below the frost and wave line
  • Ice breakers positioned to protect pilings, not just open water
  • One crew that also comes back for salvage if the lake wins
