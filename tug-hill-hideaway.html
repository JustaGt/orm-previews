The poutine that names the ride

Plenty of places on the Hill will feed you. One does poutine the way it's done north of the border — squeaky curds, proper gravy, fries that hold up under both. Groups plan their loop so lunch lands at the Hideaway.

It's been the house signature long enough that riders from Quebec nod their approval — and locals order it year-round, snow or no snow.
