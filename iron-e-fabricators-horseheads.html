What we make

Every piece is measured on site and fabricated in the shop — not ordered from a catalog. Residential, commercial and industrial.

Exterior & ramp railings

Solid iron railings for front steps and porches, plus handicap-ramp rails for homes, churches and public buildings — fit to code and to your steps.

Interior stairs & balustrades

Custom stair railings and balustrades, straight or curved, in iron or mixed metals to match the room.

Gates & fence

Driveway and garden gates, security gates and ornamental iron fence, built to the opening and hung to swing right.

Repair & restoration

Re-welding a rusted or damaged railing, re-securing loose posts, and restoring older ironwork instead of replacing it.
