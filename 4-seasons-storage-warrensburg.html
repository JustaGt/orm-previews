Boat, camper & RV storage

Warrensburg is where the trailer stops

You're 12 minutes from Lake George, half an hour from Schroon and Loon. Nobody wants to tow a 24-foot boat back down the Northway in October — and nobody wants it sitting in the side yard until May either.

Boats

Bowriders, pontoons, fishing rigs. Off the water in the fall, out of the weather all winter, ready to hook up the week the ramps open.

Campers & travel trailers

Park it here instead of the driveway. Pull it out for a long weekend at the state campgrounds and bring it right back.

RVs & motorhomes

Room to maneuver on a lot built for rigs, not a lot built for cars with a truck squeezed in.

Why Main Street

You pass us on the way in and on the way out

We're on Main Street in the middle of Warrensburg — the road everyone takes between Exit 23 and the lakes. That matters more than it sounds. Storage is only convenient if it's on a route you already drive.

Camp owners on Schroon River Road, contractors working Bolton and Chestertown, families in Thurman with one garage and two vehicles — this is the yard that's on your way, not the one that's 40 minutes the wrong direction in Queensbury.
