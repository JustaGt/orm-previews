What comes into the shop

Precision work, not a gun counter

This is bench work measured in thousandths — the kind of job you want done once, correctly. Every ticket is handled by Wade start to finish.

01

Custom rifle builds

Spec it with you barrel-up: action truing, chambering, threading, bedding and a finish that shoots to the target — not the brochure.

02

Gunsmithing & repair

Timing, headspace, worn or broken parts, feeding and extraction problems, and restoration on older and inherited firearms.

03

Barrel & accuracy work

Re-barrels, muzzle threading for a can or brake, crowning and load-workup guidance to tighten a group.

04

Machining & tool making

Lathe and mill work, one-off parts, jigs and fixtures — the same shop skills that built rifles at Remington.

05

Metal fabrication

Welding and fabrication (A.A.S. Welding Technology) for the brackets, mounts and repairs a project actually needs.

06

3D printing

Prototype parts, fixtures and hard-to-source components printed in-house before they're cut in steel.
