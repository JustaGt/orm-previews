Before you bring it in

Three things that protect your trophy

What you do in the first hours matters more than anything we do later. A quick call before you cut is always worth it.

Keep it cold

Get the animal or fish cold as soon as you can and keep it that way. Heat is what ruins a mount before it ever reaches the studio.

Don't cut the cape

For a shoulder mount, leave plenty of hide — don't cut up the neck or brisket. If you're unsure, call first and we'll walk you through it.

Bring it in soon

The sooner it's in our hands, the better the result. Drop off in Saranac Lake or call and we'll tell you the best next step.
