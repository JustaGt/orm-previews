The farm & the family

Fruit first. Winery second. Same hill for generations.

This was a fruit farm long before it held a liquor license. The family has grown berries on Payne Marsh Road for generations — the winery is what happens when a good harvest meets a restored barn and a certified farm-winery license from New York State.
