Why buy from the sugarhouse

The label on grocery syrup rarely says who made it.

Ours does. Every bottle traces back to the Ploetz woods off Dowd Road and the evaporator you can come watch run. Buying it here means it's fresh, it's local, and the money stays in Springville.

A stand you can stand behind

Making syrup since 1998, Ploetz's is the kind of small family operation that agritourism weekends were built for — a real farm, real people, and a reason to make the drive out to Erie County's hills.
