Haul the piece — or the whole implement — to the shop on Bath Road. Tell us what failed and how it's used, and we'll weld it to take the load again. Call ahead in busy season so we can fit you in.

Too big to move?

Some repairs have to happen where the equipment sits. Call and describe the job — size, location, and what broke — and we'll talk through whether it's a shop job or one we come to.
