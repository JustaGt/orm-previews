About the farm

Holly & Todd's place in the glen

Holly and Todd Jacobs bought their first two alpacas in 2006 and were hooked by the time the first cria arrived. Today the farm raises greys, blacks and every color in between on seven quiet acres, alongside sheep, chickens, ducks — and Great Pyrenees they rescue and train as guardians. Their son helps run the place.

It's a small farm on purpose. That's how every buyer gets real time, real answers, and unlimited support long after the trailer pulls away.
