What's actually here

The plain list, so nobody arrives surprised

Half of a campground's phone calls are people asking the same six questions. Answering them on the page turns those calls into bookings.

Bathhouse

Flush toilets and hot showers on the grounds. Cleaned daily through the season.

The lodge

Covered common space for rainy afternoons, card games and group gatherings.

Hookups

Electric and water on RV sites. Dump station on site — no need to break camp mid-stay.

Fire rings & wood

Ring and table at every site. Firewood sold on the grounds; please don't bring your own across county lines.

Cell & internet

Service is what it is up here. The finished page states carrier-by-carrier reality instead of promising bars.

Dogs

Leashed dogs welcome at most sites. Specific site restrictions listed on the rules page.

You are not booking a lawn. You are booking a stretch of the East Branch.

Roxbury sits high in the Catskills where the East Branch of the Delaware is still small, cold and fast. The West Settlement Stream joins it at the edge of the campground, and Stratton Falls itself is just upstream — which is why the seasonal sites on the bank get claimed a year out.

For anglers that means wadeable water without a boat. For families it means a place where kids can be in the creek all afternoon within sight of the picnic table. That's the actual product, and no generic campground template says it.

Within a short drive

  • Stratton Falls — upstream, the campground's namesake
  • Plattekill Mountain, Roxbury — biking and skiing seasons
  • Belleayre Mountain, Highmount
  • Main Street, Margaretville — groceries, hardware, the hospital
  • Catskill Scenic Trail, Grand Gorge to Bloomville
  • Pepacton Reservoir shoreline, Downsville side
Seasonal program

The campers who never leave, and why the site should sell to them first

Seasonal RV sites are the steadiest revenue a campground has. They're also the hardest thing to buy right now — there's no page explaining how it works, what it costs, or whether anything is open.

One payment, one site, all season

Your camper is placed in May and stays until October. No hitching up, no re-booking, no fighting for a spot on the Fourth of July.

Creek-side is the premium tier

A small number of sites sit directly on the water. These turn over rarely, which is exactly why a waitlist form belongs on the website.

Add cable through MTC

Seasonal campers can add TV service through MTC out of Margaretville — a small detail that matters a lot to a family spending twenty weekends here.

Returning campers get first call

Renewal happens before public release each year. The site should collect next-season interest all summer instead of in a scramble each spring.

House rules, short version

Everything people actually get wrong

Quiet hours

Late evening through early morning. Generators off during quiet hours.

Firewood

Buy it here or within the local area. Moving wood spreads pests through the Catskill forests.

Visitors

Day guests are welcome — check in at the office so we know who's on the grounds.

Check-in / out

Afternoon in, late-morning out. Early arrivals depend on the site being cleared.

Deposits

A deposit holds the site. Cancellation terms stated plainly rather than buried in a PDF.

The creek

Water levels change fast after mountain rain. Children in the stream need an adult.

Under new ownership — and that's worth saying properly

The banner on the current site mentions new ownership in passing. That's actually the most important thing a returning camper wants to know: who's running it now, what's staying the same, and what's improving. One honest paragraph here settles a year of rumours on the Roxbury Facebook groups.
