How a project runs

From shoreline visit to launch day

A big in-the-water investment should be predictable. Here's the path from first call to a dock you can walk out on.

Shoreline visit

We come out, look at your frontage, water depth, and the shape your existing dock or wall is in.

Plan & permits

You get a written scope and price; we flag any DEC/Corps permit the work needs and help file it.

Build

Barge or land crew sets the dock, drives the sheet pile, or places the riprap on the scheduled window.

Season support

Set and pull the lift, reset sections after ice-out, and handle repairs as they come up.

Permits, handled

In-water work isn't just labor — it's paperwork

Building or replacing a dock, seawall, or breakwall on Oneida Lake can trigger NYSDEC and Army Corps review. We've worked within those requirements before, so a permit question doesn't have to stall your project or land on you to sort out alone.
