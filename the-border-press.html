The family line

Set by hand in 1960. Still family-run.

1960

Jim Rochester and his father Hiram open a small letterpress shop in the village of Rouses Point, working side by side with hand-set type.

The decades between

Offset joins the letterpress. The shop becomes the printer of record for the border villages' businesses, churches, and schools.

Today

Jim's son Daniel and daughter Elaine carry the shop — digital color alongside the old presses, and the same rule the family started with: exceptional value, quality, service, and a fair price on every order.
