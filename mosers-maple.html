Our story

From Philip Moser's first taps to the sixth generation.

  • 1904 — It starts with PhilipPhilip Moser begins making syrup from the maples around Croghan.
  • Through the generationsThe sugarbush, the recipes, and the candy molds pass down hand to hand — never sold off, never franchised.
  • The candy kitchenBeyond syrup, the family builds the maple-candy and maple-cream line the North Country now knows them for.
  • Today — sixth generationStill a family-run farm with deep ties to the maple industry and the land the sap comes from.

A real producer, not a label

Plenty of maple on a shelf was bottled by someone who never tapped a tree. Moser's syrup, candy, and cream come from one family that farms the Croghan woods itself — start to finish, the way they have for over a century.

"Strong ties to the maple industry — and to the land we harvest our products from."
