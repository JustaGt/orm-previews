The Mill

Forty-plus years on Route 224

Reynolds Logging & Lumber is a small family sawmill, logging and construction operation that's been cutting in Odessa for more than forty years. Brett Reynolds runs it the way small mills have always run — on repeat customers, fair cuts and word of mouth.

What it's never had is a page online. A landowner searching "sell timber Schuyler County" or a woodworker looking for "rough-cut lumber Odessa NY" finds a Facebook page and a couple of directory stubs — nothing that explains that this mill both buys timber and saws lumber. That's the one thing this page is built to fix.
