Since 1968

Started by Jim and Beegie Miller. Still run by the family.

Fifty-plus years supplying the people who actually put boats back in the water — and a decision in 1980 that we've never walked back.

  • 1968

    Jim and Beegie Miller open the Oyster Bay Boat Shop.

  • 1980

    The retail doors close for good. From then on the shop supplies the trade only — "we don't sell to the public, our customers do."

  • 2001

    Founder Jim Miller passes away. His friends and competitors from a hundred regattas still call with stories.

  • 2002

    The shop moves to a bigger space — high ceilings, deep inventory, a rigging bench, a forklift, and neighbors who bring fabrication into the business.

  • Today

    Hardware, rigging, part books, performance gear and a machine shop, on one dealer account.
