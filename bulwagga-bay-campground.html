Hold your site

Send your dates and site type and we'll confirm availability and price. Ready to lock it in now? Call the office at (518) 546-7500, open 8am–6pm.

Arriving after hours? Your site info is posted on the Registration Building window — stop by the office the next morning to finish check-in.

When you're not on the beach

You're minutes from the lake and a short drive from the High Peaks. A few favorites:

Lake Champlain Boating

Launch, cruise the bay and drop a line — the lake is right here.

Crown Point Historic Site

Lighthouse, fort ruins and lake views a few minutes south.

Adirondack Hiking

Trails and High Peaks day trips out your campsite door.

Ferry to Vermont

Cross the lake for a day in Vermont and back by dinner.
