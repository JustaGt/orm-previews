About the name

Neverwhere is named after the Neil Gaiman novel. The owner wrote to Gaiman to ask if she could use the title, and he said yes.

The studio sits on Prairie View Circle, between Wamego and Manhattan. One chair, one client at a time, and a view of the country out the window.
