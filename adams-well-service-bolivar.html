Plugging & abandonment

Idle well on your lease? Plug it by the book.

If you own an inactive or orphaned well in the Allegany field, plugging it correctly protects your lease, your liability, and the groundwater. Adam's runs the equipment and follows the DEC and EPA procedures — cement, tagging, and the paperwork that goes with it.

We handle the whole shutdown: pulling rods and tubing, setting plugs, cementing, surface restoration, and the required reporting — so an old well stops being a problem you have to think about.
