From the shelves inside

Amish baked goods, canned goods & raw honey.

Past the produce there's a market shelf worth the stop on its own.

Amish baked goods

Pies, breads and cookies from local Amish bakers — the kind of thing that doesn't last the drive home.

Canned & preserved

Jams, pickles and put-up fruit so the orchard tastes the same in January as it does in August.

Raw local honey

Honey from right around Orleans County — pollinated, in part, by the same orchard you're standing in.

Bulk & by the case

Buying for canning, cider-pressing or a crowd? Order apples and produce by the bushel and the case.
