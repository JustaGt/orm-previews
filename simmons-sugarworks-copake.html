Our sugarbush

Sap to syrup, the slow way.

Maple isn't made in a factory. It's a few weeks in late winter, a lot of firewood, and someone watching the pan. Here's how ours comes to be.

Tapped here

The sap comes off our own maples in the Copake woods — not bought in by the barrel from somewhere else.

Boiled in small batches

Cooked down a batch at a time each spring, so nothing sits and the flavor stays bright.

Put up by hand

Bottled, baked, and jarred by the family — the same hands you'll meet at the market table.
