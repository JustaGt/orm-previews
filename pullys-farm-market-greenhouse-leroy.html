Our Three Seasons

One stand, three times a year worth stopping

A greenhouse market runs on a calendar, and Pully's fills it start to finish — from the first flats of spring to the last pumpkin of October.

Spring

THE GREENHOUSE OPENS
  • Hanging baskets & potted flowers
  • Vegetable & herb starts for your garden
  • Annuals, geraniums & bedding plants
  • Everything to plant your beds & pots

Summer

THE MARKET FILLS UP
  • Fresh-picked sweet corn
  • Summer tomatoes & garden vegetables
  • Seasonal produce as it comes ripe
  • Picked close to home, not trucked in

MUMS & PUMPKINS
