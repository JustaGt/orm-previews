Cut sheet

Decide before the day, not on the phone at 7am

Half the confusion in this business comes from people filling out a cut sheet they've never seen before. Here's what you'll actually be asked. Print it, mark it up, bring it with the animal.

Steaks

  • Thickness (¾″ · 1″ · 1¼″)
  • Per package: 2 or 4
  • T-bone or strip + fillet
  • Round steak or ground

Roasts

  • Roast weight (2–3 lb · 3–4 lb)
  • Chuck, rump, sirloin tip
  • Bone-in or boneless
  • Or convert to ground

Ground

  • Package size (1 lb · 1½ lb · 2 lb)
  • Patties: yes / no
  • Lean trim vs. all trim

Extras

  • Stew meat & short ribs
  • Soup bones · marrow bones
  • Liver, heart, tongue
  • Suet for the birds

How it works

From the phone call to the freezer

Call for a date

Kill dates book out — especially fall. Call early and we'll put you on the book for a specific day.

Bring the animal

Deliver on your date. Off feed, watered, and handled calmly — it shows up in the meat.

Turn in your cut sheet

Marked up the way you want it. If you're not sure, we'll walk you through what most people choose.

Pick up, wrapped & labelled

We call when it's ready. Everything labelled with the cut so you're not guessing in February.

Fall

Deer season

Come November, the shop's rhythm changes. If you've never brought a deer in, the rules are simple.

Bring it field-dressed and cooled. Get it here promptly — a warm deer is a wasted deer. Tell us what you want: steaks and roasts, all ground, or a mix. Tag stays with the animal.
