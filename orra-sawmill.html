How a custom saw works

  1. Call with what you've got — species, rough size, how many logs.
  2. We talk through what dimensions you want cut and roughly what it runs.
  3. Drop the logs, or bring your cut list for stock we already have.
  4. Pick up sawn lumber, stickered and ready to dry or use.

A working mill, not a warehouse

Orra Sawmill is a circle-mill operation run by Orra Brownell in Northville, at the southern edge of the Adirondack Park near the Great Sacandaga Lake. It's the kind of place you call when the box store says "we don't cut that."

Camp owners, barn builders and woodworkers around the lake come here for stock they can't buy off a rack — and to have their own timber turned into something useful.
