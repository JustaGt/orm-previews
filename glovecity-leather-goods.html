"The Glove Capital of the World"

12 Saint Thomas Place was once a bustling leather-goods factory owned by William H. Thomas, turning out wallets, purses, and travel bags. Restored in 2021, it's home again to glove making — this time by Glovecity Leather Goods.

Gloversville, New York

The craft never fully left town

For over a century, Gloversville earned its name — the gloves worn across America were cut and sewn here. When the factories closed, most of that skill scattered. Julie Grant, with fifteen years in the trade, gathered a team of Fulton County makers who still know the work and put them back to it, inside one of the old leather buildings itself.

Buying a pair isn't just buying gloves. It's keeping a genuine American craft — and the people who practice it — working in the town that invented it.
