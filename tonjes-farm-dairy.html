How We Make It

1 · Fresh morning milk

Every batch starts with milk from that morning's milking — grass-fed Holsteins raised without antibiotics or hormones.

2 · Low-temperature pasteurization

We pasteurize gently so the good qualities of the milk survive into the cup and the wheel.

3 · Milk, culture, salt. That's it.

No preservatives, additives, or stabilizers — made fresh each week since we began processing on the farm in 2002.

Three Generations on Tonjes Road

The farm has carried our family name since 1950. Tim and Mary Tonjes — with the second and third generation working side by side — do everything here: milking and animal care, haying the fields, making the products, and standing at the market tables every weekend. When you buy a cup of our yogurt, the person who made it probably handed it to you.
