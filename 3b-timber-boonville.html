Who you're dealing with

A Boonville family in the timber business since 1981

3B Timber Company was founded in 1987 by brothers Mark Bourgeois (President & CEO) and Gary Bourgeois (Vice President), who'd already been working in timber since 1981. From a red-pine pole yard it grew to manufacture utility poles, mulch, pulpwood and firewood — all processed on Industrial Drive in Boonville. We stay current on forestry management and standing-timber prices so the number we give a landowner is a fair one.
