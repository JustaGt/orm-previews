Know your grades

Same trees. Four colors of the season.

Maple grades aren't quality tiers — they're a calendar. Early-season sap runs light; late-season runs dark and bold. Pick by how you'll use it.

Golden — Delicate

First runs of the season. Pour it on vanilla ice cream and nothing else.

Amber — Rich

The pancake classic. What most people mean when they say "real maple."

Dark — Robust

Stands up in baking, glazes, and coffee. A bakers' favorite.

Very Dark — Strong

End-of-season boil. The one for barbecue sauce and beans.

What 3,000 gallons actually takes

1 · The sugarbush

8,000+ taps across the hillside above the farm, run on tubing to the sap house below.

2 · The run

When March days thaw and nights freeze, sap moves — and so does everyone with the last name Lawton.

3 · The boil

Roughly 40 gallons of sap make one gallon of syrup. The 2021 sap house was built to keep pace with the hill.

4 · The jug

Graded, filtered, and bottled on the farm at 431 Bridge Street — the same land the family has milked and tapped for decades.
