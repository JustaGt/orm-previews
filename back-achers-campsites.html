What makes it different

Actual lake frontage — not "near the lake"

Plenty of Finger Lakes campgrounds put "Seneca Lake" in the name. Back-Achers is the one private campground that's genuinely on it, with frontage that's yours to swim and dock from. That's the whole reason regulars come back summer after summer.

  • The only private campground on Seneca Lake with private frontage
  • Cottages with their own docks
  • Five miles from the Finger Lakes Wine Trail
  • A family-run, vintage-camping feel
