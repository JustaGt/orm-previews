What we do

One yard, a quarter mile from the water

The lakes off Route 43 have docks and boathouses but no full-service marina. That is the job Burden Lake Marine does: we hold the boat, we ready it, and we run it to your dock.

Dock delivery & removal

Your boat goes in when the ice is out and comes off before it freezes — you never hitch a trailer.

Indoor & outdoor storage

Winter storage for boats and cars, plus summer trailer storage so the trailer is not parked on your lawn all season.

Spring prep & checkover

Cleanup, systems check, and a boat that starts the first weekend instead of the third.

Shrink wrapping

Wrapped tight for a Rensselaer County winter, ventilated so it comes out dry.

Acid wash & hull cleaning

Waterline stain and lake scum off the hull before it sets for another year.

Routine service & insured transport

Maintenance in the yard and licensed, insured hauling anywhere in the Capital Region.
