What we do

Dirt work for hill-town homes and small commercial sites

The everyday jobs that keep a rural property working — and the ones a homeowner shouldn't hire out to a national outfit two counties away.

Driveways & access roads

New driveways cut, graded and stoned; culverts set; washed-out gravel drives rebuilt to shed water instead of trapping it.

Foundation & basement digs

Excavation for new homes, additions, garages and pole barns — dug to grade, backfilled and cleaned up.

Drainage & regrading

Wet yard, water in the basement, standing pools after every storm — regrading, swales and drain lines to move water off the property.

Land clearing & fill

Brush and stump removal, lot clearing, pond work, and bringing in or hauling out fill to level a site.
