Spring bedding plants

Raised right in our greenhouses and sold in season, so you can start your own garden with plants grown on North Fork soil.

How pick-your-own works

First time out? It's about as simple as farming gets.

1

Pull in & grab a wagon

Park at the stand on Breakwater Road. Wagons and boxes are right there — no charge to head into the fields.

2

Pick what's ready

Walk the rows and pick what looks good that day. If it's on the stand, it's ripe in the field.

3

Weigh up at the stand

Bring your box back, settle up, and take home vegetables that were in the ground twenty minutes ago.

Straw — picked up here or delivered across Long Island

We grow and bale our own straw and deliver it anywhere on the Island, year-round. It's the same clean straw the farm runs on.

Ask about a straw delivery

What folks use it for

  • Animal bedding for barns, coops & kennels
  • Fall & harvest decoration
  • Erosion control on new seed & grading jobs
  • Mulch & garden bedding

Pick up at the farm or have it delivered — available all year, not just in October.
