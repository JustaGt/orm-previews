The parts of a barn you only notice in January

A 90×180 indoor is the difference between riding all winter and not riding from December to April. That's most of what people are really shopping for.

90 × 180 indoor arena

Big enough for a real course or a full dressage court, and open when the footing outside is frozen solid.

200 × 250 outdoor arena

Room to gallop a line, set a grid, or just get a young horse out of an enclosed space.

Hay grown on the property

We cut it here. Round bales stay in the paddocks; free-choice, not rationed.

Daily turnout, no exceptions

Horses that live out are sounder and saner. Individual or group depending on your horse.

Room for every discipline

Hunters, dressage riders, trail riders and western boarders all keep horses here without stepping on each other.

140 acres of quiet

Hill views, no traffic noise, and a drive from campus that takes twelve minutes.

The back gate is the whole point

Most boarding barns near Ithaca mean loading up a trailer and driving somewhere to hack out. Here you tack up, walk out, and you're on state land.

  • Direct access to roughly 3,000 acres of state forest trail
  • Ride out after work without hitching a trailer
  • Conditioning hills for endurance and eventing riders
  • Quiet trail work for young or spooky horses
  • Somewhere to go on the days the arena feels small
If you have ever spent a Sunday morning loading a horse into a trailer just to go for a walk in the woods, you already understand what this farm is offering.

Lessons, leasing and sales

An important thing to know up front: J&B is not a public trail-ride operation. Lessons are for boarders and for riders leasing a horse here — that's how the barn stays quiet and the horses stay consistent.

Leasing a horse

Ride regularly without buying. Lessees get instruction and full use of the arenas and trails.

Lessons for leasers

Flat and jump work, tailored to the horse you're riding and where you're trying to get to.

Horses for sale

Sale horses come through the barn from time to time. Ask what's currently available.
