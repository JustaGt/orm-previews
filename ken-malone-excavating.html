Why local matters out here

Hannibal ground isn't forgiving

Heavy clay in one field, old muck and peat beds in the next, ledge where you least want it. A crew from two counties over guesses at it. We've been setting tanks and cutting drives in this exact dirt for years — we know where it drains, where it heaves, and where the perc test is going to fight you.

That local read is the difference between a septic field that lasts and one that surfaces in three springs.
