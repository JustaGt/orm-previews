What's in the rows

A working salvage yard built around the antiques

Decades of parts tractors picked, sorted, and shelved — plus new reproduction pieces for the jobs where used won't do. Here's where restorers usually start.

John Deere

Two-cylinders & crawlers

Letter-series and 10/20-series parts, plus late 440 / 1010 / 350 crawler pieces — final-drive gaskets, air stacks, castings and more.

Farmall / IH

Cub & red-power parts

Farmall Cub and International parts pulled from the yard, sorted by model so we can tell you fast whether we've got yours.

Rims & wheels

USA-made rims a specialty

Double-bevel, flat-base, power-adjust and 6-bolt rims — many USA-made — including upgrade sizes to fit larger front tires on 3010/4020s.

Sickle-bar

Mower & sickle-bar parts

Sickle-bar sections, guards and related pieces for keeping older haying equipment cutting.

Castings & hard parts

Gaskets, mufflers, air stacks

USA-made mufflers and air stacks, oil-pan gaskets, and the small castings that are impossible to find at a dealer counter.

Whole machines

Parts tractors for sale

Whole and partial tractors — buy the whole thing to part out, or let us pull what you need off it. Ask what's on the lot now.
