The Kitchen

Cooked by the chef, not a system

Behind Tra Di Noi is chef-owner Marco Coletta, who keeps the menu deliberately small so every plate leaves the kitchen the way it should. There's no factory line here — just a chef who's been doing this for years, in a dining room he runs himself.

The result earned a place in the Michelin Guide. But the room still feels like a neighborhood secret: crimson walls, red-checkered tablecloths, and regulars who book the same table every month.

The Room

Small by design

Tra Di Noi seats just a handful of tables, which is the point. It's quiet enough to hear your guest, warm enough to stay for the second bottle, and intimate enough that the chef might come by to see how dinner's going. Because of that, evenings book up — especially weekends.
