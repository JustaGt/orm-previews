How a day on the river works

1

Reserve a date

Send your date, boats and headcount below. We confirm your spot — reservations are recommended for any size group.

2

Arrive & gear up

Check in from 10am (last launch 2:30pm). We fit life jackets, hand out paddles, and run the safety briefing.

3

We shuttle you

One-way shuttle to your put-in where it applies — your car's waiting at the take-out.

4

Float & finish

Enjoy up to 4 hours on the water. All boats off the river by 6pm. That's it.

Before you book

Know the river, know the rules

A quick float-day rundown so your group shows up ready. River levels change daily — check the live flow before you come.

  • Hours start at 10am; last appointment is 2:30pm.
  • Rentals run up to 4 hours — all boats off the water by 6pm.
  • Groups of 6+ must reserve. Reservations recommended for everyone.
  • No charge for weather cancellations.
  • Overnight/camping floats available — just ask when you book.
