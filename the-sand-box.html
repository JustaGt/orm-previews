Two steps, one shop

Blast it clean. Coat it to last.

Most jobs come through here for both — strip first, then a durable powder finish. We can also do one or the other.

What we blast

Media blasting strips rust, old paint, coatings and scale back to bare, ready-to-finish metal.
  • Rusted frames, brackets & mounts
  • Old paint & flaking powder coat
  • Wheels, rims and suspension parts
  • Trailers, decks and gates
  • Hardware down to nuts & bolts

What we coat

Powder coating is electrostatically applied then oven-baked — tougher and longer-lasting than spray paint.
  • Any color — gloss, satin, matte or texture
  • Automotive & motorcycle parts
  • Patio furniture & railings
  • Equipment, brackets and fabrications
  • Single parts or full batches

The blast-media menu

Different jobs need different grit. We run the full range and pick the right one for your part.

Sand

Workhorse media for heavy rust and scale on tough steel parts.

Coal Slag

Aggressive cut for thick coatings and stubborn surface rust.

Glass Bead

Cleans and leaves a smooth satin finish without eating the metal.

Walnut Shell

Gentle organic media for softer metals and delicate parts.

Corn Cob

Low-abrasion cleaning that strips grime without warping thin stock.

Soda

The softest option — cleans without removing the base metal underneath.

How a job runs

Strip → blast → powder & bake

Strip & inspect

We look the part over, mask anything that shouldn't be coated, and pick the blast media that fits the metal.

Blast to bare metal

In the booth or cabinet, every bit of rust, paint and old coating comes off so the powder has clean metal to grip.

Powder & bake

Your color goes on electrostatically, then bakes in the 10×20 oven into a hard, even finish that won't chip like spray paint.
