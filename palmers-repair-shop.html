A dying engine isn't always a dying vehicle

Around here a good 3/4-ton with a tired motor is worth saving — a replacement costs more than most families want to spend. Palmer's is one of the few shops in Delaware County that still does engine installation and exchange in-house, listed and categorized for exactly that work.

Bring it in, get a straight answer: rebuild, exchange, or — if the frame's gone — an honest "don't put money in this one."

Before you scrap it, ask about:

  • Complete engine exchanges & installs
  • Rebuilds on trucks, vans & farm vehicles
  • Cooling-system and head-gasket work
  • Post-install break-in checks
