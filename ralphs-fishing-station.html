Since 1961

Ralph and Barbara started with rowboats and a few moorings

The peninsula the marina sits on today wasn't much more than sand and one tree when the family moved to the north side of the harbor. Everything on it since then, they built.

1961

Ralph and Barbara Davenport open a small marina on the south end of Mount Sinai Harbor, renting rowboats and a handful of moorings.

1977

The move to the north side of the harbor — a sandy peninsula with a single tree on the end of it.

Today

Still run by Ralph and Barbara's children and grandchildren. Full service yard, fuel dock, kitchen and a deck full of people on a Friday night.
