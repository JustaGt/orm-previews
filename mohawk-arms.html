What comes through the door

Historical material, correctly identified

We handle original historical military artifacts from many nations and conflicts. Our stated pride is in offering original material with correct historical identifications — which also means telling a consignor when a piece isn't what the family believed it to be.

The house

One of the oldest militaria auction houses in the country

Mohawk Arms Inc. has operated for over forty-five years and is known to militaria collectors worldwide. Our clients include museums, professional investors, serious collectors of historical artifacts, historians, and people buying their first piece. That background — years of international travel, hundreds of collections viewed, thousands of items handled — is what we bring to an identification, whether the client is a museum registrar or an insurance adjuster.
