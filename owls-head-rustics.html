Our story

Forty years of retail, brought home to Keene

After three decades running retail in Pennsylvania, Jerry and Janet retired to Janet's Adirondack roots — 72 acres at the foot of Owls Head Mountain. They renovated the 1930s log store on Route 73, then built a furniture gallery to show the Great Camp pieces this region is known for.

"We design and build our own furniture with no middle man — beautiful rustic pieces at a fair price, made to become an Adirondack memory."
