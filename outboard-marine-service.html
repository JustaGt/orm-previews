The decision

Re-power, rebuild, or walk away

There is no single right answer, and any shop that gives you one before looking at the boat is guessing. Here is roughly how we think about it when you call.

Re-power usually makes sense when

  • The hull, stringers and transom are sound.
  • You like the boat and the layout suits how you actually fish or cruise.
  • The current engine is a high-hour two-stroke or a sterndrive facing a major job.
  • Fuel burn and noise have become the reason you go out less.

It may not be worth it when

  • The transom is soft or the stringers have taken water.
  • The rigging, controls and gauges would all have to be replaced too.
  • The boat's weight capacity won't carry a modern four-stroke of the size you want.
  • You were already planning to change boats within two seasons.
