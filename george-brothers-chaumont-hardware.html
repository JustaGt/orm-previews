The story

Hardware has been sold in this village for over a century.

Chaumont has had a hardware counter since the days when goods arrived by rail and schooner, and this store carries that line forward — the same trade, the same village, generations later.

Today it runs as George Brothers Chaumont Hardware, a True Value store on Route 12E: part hardware, part tackle shop, part lumberyard, entirely local.
