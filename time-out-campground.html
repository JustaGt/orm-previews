Whether you're rolling in with a fifth-wheel or just need four walls and a roof, there's a spot for it on the north shore.

🏠

Cabins

⛺

Lean-To Sites

Half-shelter, half-outdoors — the classic Central NY camping setup for folks who want to be in it but covered overhead.

RV Sites

Pull-in sites for the camper or trailer, in sun or shade as you like it. Settle in for a weekend or stay the season.

The reason lake people stay here: the boat launch

Most campers near Oneida Lake make you trailer your boat across town to put it in. Ours is about a minute up the road — our own launch, so the boat, the kayaks and the water toys go straight in.

Fish for walleye and perch in the morning, beach it at Sylvan Beach in the afternoon, and you're back at the fire by dark.
