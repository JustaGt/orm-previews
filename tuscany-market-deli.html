The Civiletto family

A hundred years of feeding this town

The Civiletto family has been in the grocery trade in Fredonia for over a century, and Tuscany Market & Deli carries that line forward: sawdust-and-salami old-school, built on knowing customers by name and by usual order. Local writers keep "discovering" it — regulars just call it the market.
