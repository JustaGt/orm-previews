Why Young Warriors

More than a gym.

Francis didn't start this to crank out trophies. He started it because a kid with somewhere to be after school, a coach who notices when something's off, and a goal to chase tends to stay on track.

Every young boxer here learns the same thing first: you earn what you get. The footwork and the right hook come after the discipline does. That's the Middletown way we coach it.
