Who stores here

Four very different customers, four different pages

A weekender storing kayaks and a builder storing staging are not the same sale. Splitting them is the single biggest ranking and conversion gain available here.

Second-home owners

Kayaks and paddleboards in winter, skis and snowshoes in summer, spare furniture year-round. A 5×10 costs less than losing half the garage.

Contractors & trades

A locked, lit, drive-up base between jobs across Delaware County — closer than hauling everything back to the yard every night.

Moving & renovating

The gap between closing dates, or the six weeks the floors are being redone. Month-to-month means you're not signing a year for a six-week problem.

Local businesses

Terms

Month-to-month, or prepay and pay less

Month to month

Rent for as long as you need it and stop when you're done. Best for moves, renovations and jobs with an end date. No long contract, no penalty for leaving on schedule.

Prepay for longer, save more

Paying for a longer period up front lowers the monthly cost — the right choice for second-home owners and contractors who know the unit is a permanent fixture. Discount tiers are published on the finished site.

A note on portable containers

KDR no longer offers dumpsters or portable storage — that side of the business transferred to A-Verdi, and the company is now KDR Self Storage. Worth stating clearly and early, because the current site still lives on the old kdrcontainers.com address and people arrive expecting a container drop-off.
