New fabrication

Drawn, cut, welded and finished on Elm Street by craftsmen who have been doing it for decades.

  • Site measure before anything is fabricated
  • Shop drawing you sign off on
  • Finish chosen from real samples, not a screen
  • We hang it — no "installer to be arranged"

Repair & restoration

Gold Coast houses come with iron that is older than everyone in it. Broken scrolls, rusted feet, a gate that has dropped an inch, brass gone green.

  • Rusted rail feet cut out and re-set
  • Gates re-hung and re-squared
  • Brass and bronze polished and re-lacquered
  • Missing sections matched to the original pattern

Family owned, second generation

Fifty-nine years on the same lot

Joseph Gallo started Forest Iron Works on a small lot in Locust Valley in 1967. His son Mario runs it now. In between, the shop has railed, gated and restored houses, schools, hospitals and municipal buildings across Long Island and into the city — and donated a good deal of iron back to the villages that kept it going.
