How a tide trip works

One river, one direction — with the current at your back

1

Pick your window

The Nissequogue is tidal. We launch when the current is with you — downstream on the outgoing tide, upstream on the incoming. That's why times change every week.

2

We handle the logistics

Park once. Our van shuttles you and your boat so the river carries you the whole way — around 2½ hours of easy paddling, beginner-friendly.

3

Drift the marsh

Osprey, herons, fiddler crabs, and a pristine salt marsh most Long Islanders never see. Bring a camera in a zip-top bag. You'll want it.

The Nissequogue

Long Island's quietest 5½ miles

From the launch to the Sound, the river winds through the Nissequogue River State Park — the old Kings Park waterfront most people only ever drive past. No motors roaring by. No crowds. Just tide, grass, and birds.

"Abundant bird life and a pristine salt marsh environment" isn't marketing — it's the actual view from the boat.
  • Wildlife: osprey, great blue herons, egrets, diamondback terrapins
  • Difficulty: easy — the current does the work when you time the tide
  • Trip length: ~2½ hours, 5½ miles
  • Season: spring through fall, tide-dependent
