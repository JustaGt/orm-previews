What we can take on

Structural Steel

02
Fabricated Plate

How a job runs

One shop owns it from print to punch list

The reason plants keep the work here: nothing gets handed off to a separate detailer, a separate fab shop and a separate erector. Four stages, one phone number.

PHASE 01

Design

We sit down with your drawing, sketch or site measure and pin down exactly what gets built.

PHASE 02

Engineer

Connections, loads and details worked out so the steel is right before material is cut.

PHASE 03

Fabricate

Cut, formed, fit and welded on our Fulton floor — tracked to your job, not stacked in a queue.

PHASE 04

Install

Our crew delivers and sets it in place, then walks the punch list with you.
