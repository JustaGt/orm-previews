Bring your own log

Got a tree down? We'll mill it.

A storm-dropped oak, a maple from the yard, a walnut you've been saving — don't let it rot or go to the chipper. Haul it to Kingsbury Road and we'll saw it into slabs or boards you can actually use.

You keep the wood that grew on your own land, cut to the thickness you want. It's the kind of job a portable mill down the road can't always handle and a lumberyard won't touch.
