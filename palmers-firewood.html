WHY SEASONING MATTERS

Wet wood costs you twice

Green wood doesn't just light hard — it sends a third of its heat up the chimney as steam and leaves creosote behind. Seasoned wood gives you more heat per cord and a cleaner flue. That's the whole reason to buy from someone who dries it properly.

Fully seasoned~18%
