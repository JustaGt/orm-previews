Up for the weekend?

Bring the dog to Ellicottville. Ski anyway.

Half the dogs on our floor belong to people who came up for the mountain and didn't want to leave a dog crated in a rental all day. Here's how a visitor day works.

Book before you drive

Call or text (716) 801-1471 with your dates. Daycare runs Tuesday through Friday — weekend visitors should ask us early, since weekend care is by arrangement.

Send vaccination records

Rabies, distemper and bordetella from your vet, emailed ahead so drop-off is a handshake and not paperwork.

Drop off, then go

Killbuck Road is a short run from the village and the slopes. Pick up by 5:00pm, tired dog, no guilt.

The chew bar

The lobby is a small shop, on purpose

Grab-and-go single-ingredient chews — bully sticks, beef tendon, trachea — plus skin, coat and allergy supplements, freeze-dried liver, Norwegian salmon oil, kongs, peanut butter and rechargeable LED collars for the dogs walking dark roads in December. Prices in-store; ask us to hold something with a pickup.
