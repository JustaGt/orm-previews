On the square in Pella

Windmill Cafe sits at 709 Franklin Street, right on the square in Pella.

Breakfast is served all day, with daily specials, homemade soup and pie, and a brunch buffet on Sundays. The kitchen cooks American and Greek food side by side.

It carries the Best Breakfast award from Best of Red Rock, and the doors open at 5:30 in the morning six days a week.

Good to know

Is breakfast really served all day?

Yes. Breakfast is served all day, every day the doors are open.

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes. Sunday brunch runs from 8 AM to 1:30 PM.

What is there besides breakfast?

Tenderloins, gyros, sandwiches, salads, homemade soup and a daily special, plus Greek specialties from the kitchen.

Is there dessert?

Homemade pie and homemade desserts. Save room.

Where are you?
