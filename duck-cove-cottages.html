Your boat on your own dock, ten steps from the porch.

Duck Cove is a small colony of housekeeping cottages on a quiet cove of the St. Lawrence. Every cottage comes with its own private dock, there's a boat ramp right on the property, and the fishing grounds of the Alexandria Bay corridor are minutes away by water.

The Duck Cove difference

Every cottage has its own private dock.

No shared slips, no dragging gear across a marina lot. Tie up at your cottage, clean your catch, and be back on the water before the coffee's cold. The on-site ramp means you launch once and you're set for the week.

  • Private dock with every cottage
  • Boat ramp on the property
  • Protected cove water off the main channel
  • Smallmouth, pike and walleye grounds minutes away

Inside the cottages

Set up for a real week of river living.

Full kitchens

Electric range and oven, full-size refrigerator, microwave, coffee maker, pots, pans, dishes and tableware for the whole cottage — cook your catch, skip the restaurant line.

Comfort, all season

Air conditioning for July, electric heat for the crisp May and September weeks, cable TV and fiber internet when the weather sends you indoors.

Outdoor living

Gas grill and picnic table at every cottage. Dinner is whatever came off the water, cooked twenty feet from where you caught it.

Bring your own bed linens and towels — Duck Cove is a housekeeping colony, and that's part of how the weekly rate stays sensible.

How a river week works

Saturday to Saturday, May to the end of September.

Saturday, 3:00 pm

Check in, back the trailer down the ramp, tie up at your dock. The week starts on the water, not in a lobby.

The week between

Fish the morning bite, run to Alexandria Bay by boat or car, grill at dusk. Cottages are self-catering — clean on arrival, left clean on departure.

Saturday, 10:00 am

Pull the boat, say your goodbyes. Most guests book the same week for next year before they leave the driveway.
