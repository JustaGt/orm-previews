Getting here & what to bring

  • Dress warm — sturdy boots for the field and the lot
  • We supply the saws and the baling; you supply the helpers
  • Bring twine or a roof rack if you're tying on top — we can help load
  • Come early on December Saturdays for the best pick
  • Cash and card both work — ask about custom wreath orders
