Quick answers

How often should a cesspool be pumped?

For most Suffolk households, every 2–3 years — sooner with heavy use, a garbage disposal, or slow drains. If you can't remember your last pump-out, it's time.

Do you handle emergencies on weekends?

Yes — service runs 7 days a week. Call (631) 924-4813 and talk to a person.

How many portable toilets does my event need?

A good rule: one unit per 50 guests for a 4-hour event. Tell us the headcount and hours and we'll size it for you.
