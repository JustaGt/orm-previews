Three Generations

A Sylvan Beach story that starts with a hot dog stand.

1934

Eddie Stewart Sr. and his wife Fifi come to Sylvan Beach for the sunshine and the water — opening what becomes Eddie's Restaurant, and beginning the family's nine decades on this shore.

The cottages

The family restores historic Sylvan Beach cottages and adds newly built units, so the families who grew up here can keep coming back — now as Sunset Cottages.

Today

Three generations later, the Stewarts still keep the cottages the way regulars remember them — and first-timers become regulars.
