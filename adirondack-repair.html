What comes in the door

Most of it falls into four buckets. If yours doesn't, call anyway — we'd rather tell you it's not our work than have you tow it twice.

Hydraulics

Loaders that won't lift, slow or drifting cylinders, leaking hoses and fittings, pump and valve problems, quick-couplers that won't take an attachment.

Drivetrain & final drives

Chain cases, drive motors, clutches, PTO trouble, transmissions that grind or won't pull.

Engines & fuel

Hard starting, no-start, diesel fuel and injection issues, cooling and overheating, glow plugs, belts and general tune-up.

Electrical & controls

Charging and starting faults, safety switches and interlocks that shut the machine down, wiring chewed up by mice and winter.

In the shop, or out at the machine

Some jobs need a bench. Some jobs need a truck in your field. Say which one you think you've got and we'll tell you if you're right.
