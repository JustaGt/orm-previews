Geothermal

Thinking about a ground-source heat pump? The hole in the ground is our job.

Geothermal heating starts with properly drilled, properly grouted bores — sized to your system, sealed to protect the aquifer. We work alongside your heat-pump installer and handle the drilling and grouting end to end.

If you're weighing geothermal for a Columbia County home, talk to us before the design is final. Where the bores go — and what the ground under your lot is made of — changes the plan.

We are dedicated to the exploration, development and conservation of groundwater — our most valuable natural resource. Every well and system we install meets or surpasses applicable regulatory standards.
