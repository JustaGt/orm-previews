What caretaking covers

Care that follows the Adirondack calendar

Your camp needs different things in February than it does in July. Here's the kind of work that keeps a second home sound through every season — built around your property, not a checklist.

Winter

Snow & storm watch

  • Driveway and walkway snow removal before you arrive
  • Roof snow load cleared before it becomes a problem
  • Interior checks so a heat or pipe issue is caught early
  • A look over the property after every big storm
Spring

Opening & clean-up

  • De-winterizing and getting water back on
  • Spring clean-up of grounds and walkways
  • Catching what winter left behind — limbs, leaks, critters
  • Getting the camp ready before your first weekend up
Summer

Season upkeep

  • Routine summer maintenance while you come and go
  • Firewood cut and stacked, pellets picked up and delivered
  • Small repairs and home-improvement projects handled
  • A point person on the ground when you can't be there
Fall

Closing up right

  • End-of-season clean-up and closing the camp down
  • Winterizing so nothing freezes while you're gone
  • Securing the property for the months ahead
  • A clear report on how the camp was left
