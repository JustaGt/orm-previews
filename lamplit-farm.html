Ride here or ride there

Your venue, or the farm on Carey Hill.

Every booking is priced individually — by distance, time, and what you have in mind. Rides at the farm come with views that reach the Oneida Lake basin and, on the clearest days, Lake Ontario.

On-farm rides include the three-season pavilion:
  • Wood stove going for cold-weather rides
  • Handicapped-accessible bathroom
  • Refrigerator & picnic tables for your party
  • Room to linger before and after your ride
