The Bench

Canvas work for Sodus Bay boats

Repairs & re-stitching

UV-rotted thread is usually what fails first — the fabric outlives its stitching by years. Seams re-sewn before a small opening becomes a torn panel.

Zipper replacement

Corroded sliders and blown zipper tape on enclosures, dodgers and camper backs replaced, so you stop wrestling the same stuck zipper every launch and haul-out.

Cockpit covers

Small-boat cockpit covers built to your boat's measurements — keeping rain, leaves and gulls out of the boat all season at the slip.

Mooring covers

Mooring covers made and repaired for the winter that Lake Ontario actually delivers — snow load, wind and six months on the hard.

Repair First

The repair-first arithmetic

Repair it

New stitching, a new zipper or a patched panel is usually a fraction of replacement cost — and your cover already fits your boat, because it was made for it.

Replace it

Full new canvas runs into four figures fast and takes weeks in peak season. Worth it when fabric is truly gone — and we'll tell you honestly when it is.
