The base is the part you never see — and the reason it lasts.

Anyone can stack stone for a season. A wall that stays plumb and a patio that doesn't heave come from the excavation, the compaction, and the drainage underneath. That's where the real work goes.

The approach

High-end stonework, done by the people who'll be on site

Diamond Rock takes on a measured number of projects so each one gets the attention fine masonry needs. Denis is on the job, setting stone and making the calls — not running between six sites.

From dry-laid fieldstone walls to cut-bluestone patios and full stone veneer, every piece is selected and placed for the way it sits with the next. The result reads as one composition, not a pile of rock.
