One shop, four phases

Every job moves through the same sequence — so what shows up on your site matches what was on your print.

PHASE 01

Design & detailing

We work from your drawings or develop the detail with you — connections, tolerances and code items resolved before any steel is cut.

PHASE 02

Fabrication

Structural and miscellaneous steel cut, fit and assembled in our Homer shop, on US Route 11 minutes from I-81.

PHASE 03

Certified welding

Shop and field welding across the range a fifty-year fab shop sees — from machine-part buildups to stair stringers to building frames.

PHASE 04

Field services & erection

Our crews deliver, set and erect — and come back for the repairs and modifications your equipment needs over its life.

What leaves this shop

Stairs, rails & fire escapes

Straight-run and spiral stairs, handrail, guardrail and fire escapes fabricated to code for commercial and residential work.

Component & machine parts

One-off and repeat parts for manufacturers — designed, fabricated and repaired so the line keeps moving.

Buildings & structures

Structural frames and complete buildings of all types, fabricated in-shop and erected in the field by our own people.

Stadium & specialty steel

Stadium seating, stairwells and the odd jobs other shops pass on — if it's steel, we've probably built one.

Equipment repair

Repair of equipment of all types — agricultural, industrial and municipal — welded right the first time.

Field welding services

On-site welding when the work can't come to the shop, scheduled around your downtime.

Fifty years of repeat customers
