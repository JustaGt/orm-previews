Straight answers

Open cell or closed cell? Depends on the building.

Open cell

Lighter, more affordable per inch, excellent air-seal and sound-deadening for interior walls and ample attic depths.

  • Best for: attics, interior walls, sound control
  • Expands to fill irregular framing

Closed cell

Dense, moisture-resistant, and roughly R-6 to R-7 per inch — the right call where space is tight or damp is a threat.

  • Best for: rim joists, crawl spaces, pole barns, exterior assemblies
  • Adds structural rigidity
