Our farm

Two people, a small herd, and a dairy we built ourselves.

Heather Kamin and Jon Franklin have been milking Alpine goats on Cotton Hill since 2010, and licensed for commercial cheese-making since 2014. The dairy is small on purpose — it's the two of us and the goats, and that's why every batch tastes like this hill and not a factory somewhere.

We manage the pasture without synthetic fertilizers, pesticides or herbicides, because the milk starts in the grass.
