How picking works here

First time out? It’s simple, it’s cash-or-check, and the kids are welcome in the rows.

1

Check the board or call

See what’s ripe today and whether we’re open. Weather and the crop set the hours — a quick call saves the trip.

2

Bring containers & cash

Buckets, bags, or an ice-cream pail all work. We take cash and check at the stand. Wear closed shoes for the rows.

3

Pick your own

We’ll point you to the trees that are picking best that morning. Pick as much or as little as you like.

4

Weigh & pay

Priced by the pound at the stand. Can’t stay to pick? We keep some already-picked out front while it lasts.

Get the “cherries are ready” text

The whole trick with cherries is timing — they’re only perfect for about ten days. Drop your name below and we’ll message you the morning each crop opens and when it’s about to picked out, so you never miss the window or drive out for nothing.

Just crop updates from the farm. No spam, and we never share your number.

Make a morning of it on the grape belt

We sit right on the Route 20 corridor between the lake and the wineries — an easy stop on a Chautauqua-Lake-Erie day.

Come early

Cherries pick best in the cool of the morning and the rows are quiet. Bring the kids before the day heats up.

Pair it with the wineries

You’re minutes from the Lake Erie wine trail. Pick a few pounds of cherries, then work your way down Route 20.

Freeze & bake later

Pit and freeze what you pick, or turn the tart cherries into pie. Ask us how the canners in the area put theirs up.
