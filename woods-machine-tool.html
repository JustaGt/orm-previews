Sending a job

Three ways in. All of them end in a quote.

1

You have a print

PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP, or a photo of a paper drawing on the bench. Upload it with the material, the quantity and the date. That's the whole job.

2

You have the broken part

No drawing, no part number, no supplier left. Bring it in or send photos with a rough dimension and we'll work back from the part itself.

3

You'd rather just talk

Call (607) 699-3253 between 7am and 3pm, Monday to Friday, and describe it. Howell Street is a short run off Route 17C.

Two kinds of work

The rush job and the run

The rush job

Something snapped, the line is down, and the part hasn't been made since 2004. Bring the broken one. A shop that can work from a failed part instead of a drawing is worth more than a shop with a nicer website — and that is exactly the work this one has taken for forty years.

The production run

Repeat parts, held to print, quoted per piece with a real lead time. Send the drawing once; reorders come off the same setup. This is the work the shop wants more of, and the reason the quote form is the first thing on the page.
