Where you are

Hidden, but close to everything

The name fits — you're off the main road, surrounded by water and trees. But you're minutes from the things that make a canal trip easy, and a short cruise from open lake water in either direction.

  • On the Cayuga-Seneca Canal, just west of Lock 4 in Waterloo
  • Open water to Seneca Lake to the west and Cayuga Lake to the east
  • Minutes to Seneca Falls & Geneva for supplies and dinner
  • Near Waterloo Premium Outlets & del Lago when you want off the boat
  • A quiet base for exploring the Finger Lakes by water
