The kitchen

Cooked like home, not a chain

Warique means "the hidden gem" — the little neighborhood spot locals keep to themselves. That's exactly what this is: a tight, honest Peruvian kitchen in the heart of Jackson Heights.

The menu is small on purpose so every plate comes out right. Come hungry, bring people, and let us feed you the way we'd feed family.
