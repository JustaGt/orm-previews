About Julie

Inclusive isn't a buzzword here.

I started teaching in Kingston because so many people told me they'd quit yoga after one intimidating class. That's not what happens in this room.

Every pose comes with real options, props are out before you arrive, and you'll never be the only person figuring it out. Whether you're recovering, beginning, or coming back after years away, the mat next to mine is open.

Small classes, honest pacing, and a teacher who actually watches the room.
