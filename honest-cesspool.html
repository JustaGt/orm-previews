What we handle

Everything below the lawn, one number to call

On Long Island's sandy soil almost every older home runs on a cesspool or drywell — not town sewer. Here's what a Sayville homeowner actually needs done.

🚽

Cesspool pumping

Routine pump-outs and cleanouts to keep the system flowing before it backs up into the house. We check the ring and structure while we're in there.

💧

Drywells & drainage

Overflow drywells, roof-runoff pits and yard drainage that's stopped taking water — pumped, cleaned, and assessed for whether it needs more than a pump.

🧹

Line & drain cleaning

Sluggish or clogged main lines cleared, so the whole system drains the way it should instead of just buying you a week.

⏱

Emergency service

Slow drains, gurgling toilets, a wet spot in the yard — the calls that can't wait. Tell us what you're seeing and we'll get to you.

🏠

Real-estate inspections

Buying or selling a South-Shore home? We locate, open, and report on the cesspool so the deal doesn't stall on an unknown system.

🔎

Straight answers

Not sure if it's the cesspool, a drywell, or just a clog? We'll tell you which — before anyone starts quoting a full replacement.
