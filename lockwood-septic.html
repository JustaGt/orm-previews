Four signs it's time to call today

Catch these early and it's a routine pump. Ignore them and it's a flooded basement or a field you have to dig up.

Slow drains

Sinks, tubs and toilets all draining slow at once usually means the tank — not the pipe.

Odor outside

A sewage smell near the tank or field is the first warning most people ignore.

Wet, lush grass

A soggy or extra-green patch over the leach field means effluent is surfacing.

Gurgling

Toilets or drains that gurgle when other fixtures run mean the tank is full.
