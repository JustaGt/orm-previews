The Potter Hill Barn
Open every day · 10–5:30
Country Primitive Home Accents · Since 1994

A barn full of nooks, and every nook worth the stop.

On Route 7 between Troy and Bennington, Christine and her mom Carol have spent 31 years filling The Potter Hill Barn with country primitive home accents, handmade goods from local crafters, and seasonal rooms that change with the calendar. Give yourself time — regulars always do.

What fills the barn

Made-in-USA wherever we can find it, and locally made wherever we can get it.

For the windows & floors

Window coverings, braided and hooked rugs, and the lighting that makes a farmhouse room glow instead of glare.

Furniture & pottery

Country primitive furniture, redware and stoneware pottery, iron hooks and hangers — the bones of the look.

Candles, soaps & spices

Local-crafter candles, small-batch soaps and skin care, and pantry spices you won't find at a box store.

Gifts & little finds

Decorative signs, jewelry, toys, puzzles, books, and true-to-life florals. Gift cards available for the impossible-to-shop-for.

The barn changes with the season

Inventory turns over all year — what you saw in October won't be what you find in April.

Spring

Florals, garden accents, and fresh room refreshes after mud season.

Summer

Porch and patio primitive, Americana for the Fourth, day-tripper favorites.

Harvest

The barn at its coziest — pumpkins, lanterns, and fall-scented everything.

Christmas

The season regulars wait for. Rooms of ornaments, greens, and gifts from local hands.
