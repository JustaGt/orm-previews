    A hands-on family barn since 2003

    Built by a family who's still in the barn every day

    Since the farm was finished in 2003, Springerle Stables has been a hands-on, family-run operation with a simple emphasis: a safe, friendly place to board, train and ride. That's held true whether you're leading a pony for the first time or schooling for a show.

    Between our hunter/jumper roots, a dressage trainer on site, and a real polo program, there aren't many barns near Auburn that offer this much in one place.
