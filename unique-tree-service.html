What we take care of

Tree Removal

Pruning & Trimming

Deadwood out, crowns thinned, limbs lifted off roofs and drives — keeping the healthy trees healthy and the falling branches off your shingles.

Storm Damage Response

Stump & Brush Cleanup

What happens when the crew shows up

The look

Doc walks the tree with you — what's dead, what's dropping, what it will take. You get a straight quote on the spot.

The plan

Rigging and drop zones worked out before a saw starts — house, wires, garden, and fence accounted for.

The work

Piece by piece if it's tight, whole if it's clear — decades of judgment about which is which.

The cleanup

Wood cut and stacked or hauled, brush chipped, lawn raked. The tree is gone; the mess doesn't move in.
