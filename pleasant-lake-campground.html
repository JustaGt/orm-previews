The Lake

The reason it's called Pleasant.

Swim

A private lake without the public-beach crowds — easy entry, kid-friendly water, and space to float all afternoon.

Fish

Cast off the shore before breakfast. Panfish and bass keep young anglers busy all weekend.

Sit still

Coffee at the water's edge, loons at dusk. Nobody's in a hurry here — that's the point.

Around Camp

Little store, big porch, easy weekends.

The camp store covers what you forgot — ice, firewood, s'more supplies. Bathhouses are kept clean (campers say so in their reviews, not just us), laundry's on-site, and the Wigwam Drive loop is quiet by ten.

Coming for the New York State Fair?

The fairgrounds are an easy drive from Pennellville — camp here, skip the fairground lots, and come home to the lake each night. Fair-week sites are limited; book ahead.
