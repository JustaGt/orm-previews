Boarding

Your horse, looked after like ours.

Every boarded horse at Fisher Farms gets a 10×12 matted stall, generous turnout, and a feed plan built around its own needs — not a one-scoop-fits-all barn routine.

Full Board

  • 10×12 matted stall, cleaned daily
  • Individual feed plan — hay & grain to your horse's needs
  • Daily turnout on rotated pastures
  • Blanketing, holding for vet & farrier
  • Full use of indoor, outdoor, round pen & trails

Partial Board

  • Same stall, same arenas, same trails
  • You handle a share of the daily care
  • A practical option for hands-on owners
  • Multiple tack rooms — your gear stays organized
