The Point

750 feet of shoreline that's yours for the week

  • Two private sand beaches — shallow, sandy entries where you can watch the kids from an Adirondack chair.
  • The nightly campfire — at the waterside lean-to, facing dead west at Owls Head Mountain. Bring marshmallows; the sunset's included.
  • Docks for your boat — tie up steps from your cabin and be fishing before breakfast.
  • Four acres of room — space between cabins, not a parking lot with buildings on it.

Beyond the Point

A week on Long Lake writes its own itinerary

Fourteen miles of lake

Kayak, canoe or SUP rentals in the village — paddle out of sight of a single building within twenty minutes.

Hop a float plane

Long Lake is one of the last places you can walk into town and take off from the water for a High Peaks flightseeing run.

Mt. Sabattis, half a mile off

Trails, tennis, pickleball and basketball just past the ice cream stand — the village is a short stroll from your deck.

Basecamp for the High Peaks

Day-hike a high peak or a family-friendly nature trail and be back for the campfire — Long Lake sits in the center of everything.
