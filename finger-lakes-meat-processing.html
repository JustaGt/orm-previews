About

Rick Whitmore has been cutting meat for over thirty years.

Rick started as a meat-cutting apprentice at eighteen. In 1991 he took over Bill's Meat Market in Waterloo and ran it for ten years — and it was Bill's customers who kept asking the same question: "When are you going to open a custom slaughterhouse?"

In 2001 he answered it, opening Finger Lakes Meat Processing in Romulus so the whole job — slaughter through smokehouse — could happen under one roof, done by the same hands.

Farms around the county trust the work enough that some post our cutting instructions on their own websites for their beef customers.
