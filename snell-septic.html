How to know it's time

You don't want to learn your tank is full on Thanksgiving. Here's when to pick up the phone before it becomes an emergency.

Every 2–3 yearsAverage family home should pump on a schedule, not wait for a warning sign.
Before you sellLine up the inspection early so a septic surprise doesn't hold up your closing.
Slow drains & odorGurgling toilets, slow drains or a smell in the yard mean call now, not next month.
