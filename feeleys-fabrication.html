What comes off the table

Two things most local welders won't touch

Plenty of shops MIG mild steel and call it a day. On a lake-and-farm shoreline like Newfane and Olcott, the jobs that actually pile up are aluminum — and the ones too big or too stuck to haul in. Those are the two Patrick built the shop around.

Specialty

Aluminum welding

Aluminum boats and jon boats, dock frames and ladders, pontoon parts, rails and brackets, trailers and ramps — TIG and spool-gun work that a steel-only shop turns away. If it's the light silvery metal that "won't weld," it's what he does.

On-site

Mobile welding

Some jobs can't move — a gate post, a bucket on a machine, structural steel already bolted in place, a trailer stuck on the lot. The mobile rig comes to your dock, farm, or job site, fully insured, and fixes it where it sits.
