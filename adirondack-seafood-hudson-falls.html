Our story

Run by a fisherman, not a distributor

Adirondack Seafood started in 1989 selling fish to upstate restaurants and hotels. Over the years it grew into what it is now — a full fish market and a Maine-style seafood restaurant where locals can buy the same quality the chefs do.

Owner Mike Willig grew up in the seafood business and worked as a commercial fisherman in Alaska and New England. He knows how fish should be handled from the moment it leaves the water, and he's usually right there to help you pick a fillet or track down something hard to find for a holiday.

"We buy from small, short-trip boats on the New England coast — the same high-quality sources we started with in 1989."

— The Adirondack Seafood approach to sourcing
