What comes off our saw

Lumber for barns, pole buildings, and farm repairs

The mill

A working sawmill on this creek for over two centuries

A mill has run beside the Genegantslet Creek in Greene since 1802 — through floods, fires, and a string of owners who kept the saw turning. The Beardsley family has run it since 2018, cutting local hemlock the honest way and selling it to neighbors, builders, and farms across the region.
